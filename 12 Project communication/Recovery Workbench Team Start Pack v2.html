SCB COLLECTIONS AND RECOVERY

Recovery Workbench team start pack

Henka Group, 28 October 2026. (C) Siam Commercial Bank and Henka Pte Ltd

The Recovery Workbench is the system that owns every recovery case from 90 days past due to close, for Mortgage, Hire Purchase and SSME. It is built as the recovery module of ACE, the bank's collection engine, so collection and recovery work in one application with one case view.

From day 90 to closeOne case per customer and product, with one owner, one clock and one history
Mortgage, Hire Purchase, SSMEEvery route: restructure, settlement, deed in lieu, guarantee claim, litigation, asset disposal, debt sale and write-off
Built on ACEOne service, one repository and one front end, with data through COLLINS

Rules

Rule
1No more than three planning or review meetings a week for a squad member, and none longer than 30 minutes
2The sponsor review and the steering committee look back at finished work and are outside that limit
3Work flows on a Kanban board with limits on work in progress per column and a written pull policy
4The monthly release is the only date the business sees. What slips moves to the next epic; the date does not move
5Planning happens daily at the board. There is no sprint commitment and no velocity target

Decisions

StepWhat happens
1 RaiseAnyone raises a question the build cannot answer. The product owner adds it to the decision log with an ID
2 FrameThe analyst writes the question, the options, a recommendation, what it blocks and what happens if it is late
3 AssignThe product owner names one owner and a date needed. Decision owners are bank roles
4 Build on the recommendationThe squads build on the recommended answer and mark it as an assumption in the story
5 DecideThe owner decides within five working days of the date needed, or the decision goes to the next review
6 RecordThe decision moves to Decisions taken with the date, who took it and what it means for the build
7 ApplyDocuments and stories built on the assumption are updated. An architecture decision also gets a decision record
