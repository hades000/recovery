# 3. Approval path

- The business lead drafts the wording in Thai and English for each template version.

- Legal approves every template; Compliance also approves customer wording.

- The reference data administrator loads the version with its valid from date.

- A withdrawn template holds generation until a new version is approved (R-LIT-04).

# 4. Rules for the wording

- Plain Thai and English; the amount and the date in the first paragraph.

- Every customer letter states how to pay, how to contact the bank and how to complain.

- Demand and recall notices state the amounts by component and the demand fee as the Debt Collection Act requires.

- No threat, no shaming wording, nothing that discloses the debt to a third party.
