## Slide 2

The operating model on one page

2

Product heads Mortgage, Hire Purchase, SSME Own cures, realized recovery and cost to collect for their product. Approve above set limits.

Shared operations Collections and recovery for all products Carry out the work: case ownership, restructuring, legal, NPA, debt sale, write-off and partner management. Approve within set limits.

Center of excellence Strategy, analytics, testing, QA, rules Set strategy, rules and limits as data. Propose rule and agent changes to the strategy and model committee.

Platform EFS, ACE and the Workbench, COLLINS, agents, data Run the systems and data the work depends on. Approve system and agent releases.

| Design choice | What it means |
|---|---|
| One owner from day 90 to close | A case owner holds the case through every route, with specialists working tasks on it |
| Limits held as data | Who may approve what is the delegation of authority table, checked by the Workbench |
| People decide, the Workbench prepares | Rules and agents gather, check and recommend; people approve and handle exceptions |

## Slide 8

Decision rights

8

Approval levels follow the bank regulation on approval authority for problem loans and assets for sale: Board, Executive Committee, Problem Asset Resolution Committee, Head of Recovery function, recovery executive at division level and at department level. Holders and limits are DL18.

| Decision | Who decides |
|---|---|
| Route for a case (D6) | Case owner within the limit; the approval level for the amount above it |
| Restructure, settlement, deed in lieu | Restructuring officer within the limit; approval levels under the delegation of authority above it |
| Next legal step (D7) | Head of Litigation's team, with the enforcement owner after judgment |
| Disposal and floor price (D8) | NPA and asset manager proposes; the approval level for the amount approves the floor |
| Write off or pursue (D9) | Write-off committee on the case prepared by Credit Administration |
| Rules, limits and templates | Center of excellence proposes; strategy and model committee approves; Risk signs where a limit moves |
| Agent autonomy level | Strategy and model committee, on the measured results |

## Slide 10

Spans and layers in the target model

10

| Design rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| Teams are sized from caseload | A team's size is the caseload cap per role times the cases in its queues. The caps are DL23 |
| Leaders lead people | A team manager coaches, rebalances queues and handles escalations. Allocation is done by rules |
| Limits belong to roles, not layers | An officer approves within the limit set for the role. Higher amounts go to the approval level, not up the line |
| Specialists work tasks on the case | Restructuring, legal, NPA and debt sale officers work their tasks on the owner's case. The case does not move between teams |
| One queue per team and role | Each queue belongs to one team and one role, with an overflow queue and a service level per state (DL22) |
| Layers from the head of recovery | Function head, unit head, team manager, officer: four layers for every recovery team, proposed |

## Slide 11

What changes from today

11

| Area | Today | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership | The case moves between units, with hand-overs by email and file | One owner from day 90 to close; specialists work tasks on the case |
| Allocation | Managers allocate from spreadsheets | Rules allocate; managers rebalance and handle exceptions |
| Preparing decisions | Officers gather data from several systems and write account plans | The Workbench gathers the facts, the expected return by route and a recommendation |
| Approvals | By email and paper, with limits checked by hand | Routed to the approval level, limit checked by the Workbench, logged once |
| Documents | Prepared, checked and couriered by document services | Generated, read and indexed by the document hub; people review the exceptions |
| Appraisals | Each appraisal checked by an officer | Rule check, the agent flags outliers, an officer accepts |
| Partners | Managed by email and spreadsheet | Instructions, results and fees in the partner portal, scored each month |
| Measures | Assembled by hand for each report | Read from the decision log per decision |
