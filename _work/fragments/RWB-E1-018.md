### 1. Story

As a **product owner**, I want **every account at 90 days past due or more in the ACE account view that is not excluded placed on one recovery case per customer and product, with a case ID in the form RW-YYYY-NNNNNN issued at creation and never reused, a status and its linked accounts held by ACE business key, created by rule when the ACE ALS batch for the business date is complete**, so that **every team, letter and document refers to one case ID**.

### 2. Business rationale

Recovery teams keep their own lists today, so one account can be on several trackers under different references, and some accounts at 90 days past due have no owner at all (control CTL-01 answers this audit finding). Under D-RCV-35 the Workbench loads no source data. ACE ingests the ALS account and the SCBPDM customer, and P3 reads them through `v_ace_account`, `v_ace_customer` and `v_ace_ca_relation`. The Workbench flags for death, bankruptcy and fraud sit in `party_ext`, and the open dispute flag sits in `account_ext`. This story creates the case by rule when the ALS batch for the business date is COMPLETED in `v_ace_batch_status`. It blocks excluded accounts with a logged reason, and gives the case an ID that every later document carries. It meets REQ-CWC-001 to REQ-CWC-005 and REQ-CWC-016. It supports reconciliation REC-01 (accounts at 90 days past due against open cases, zero unexplained) and REC-05 (every `account_key`, `person_key` and `primary_person_key` on a case resolves to its ACE view row).

### 3. Preconditions

- RWB-E1-001 is done: `recovery_case`, `case_account`, `case_party`, `case_entry_exclusion`, `party_ext` and `account_ext` exist in the V0001 baseline.
- RWB-E1-069 is done: the Section A views exist, including `v_ace_account`, `v_ace_customer` and `v_ace_ca_relation` (V0002). `v_ace_batch_status` is a Section B view (V0003, after RWB-ACE-001). Until it exists the job flag stays off (DL43).
- RWB-E1-015 is done: AceDataReady (EV-26) is raised from a COMPLETED row in `v_ace_batch_status`, and AceDataLate (EV-27) when the batch is late or failed.
- RWB-E1-016 is done: the account, arrears and party data products are readable by SY-CASEWF through the P1 published interface, which reads the views by business key.
- RWB-E1-027 is done: the decision log writer is available.
- RWB-E1-042 is done: parameter `case.entry.dpd` = 90 is in force (DL16).
- The exclusion rows (rule table EXCLUSION) are loaded with reason codes DECEASED, FRAUD, BANKRUPTCY, ACTIVE_PLAN and OPEN_DISPUTE (REQ-CWC-004). Their inputs are `v_ace_account.tdr_flag`, `v_ace_customer.deceased_flag`, `account_ext.dispute_open`, and `party_ext.deceased_override`, `party_ext.bankruptcy` and `party_ext.fraud_flag`, all read by business key.
- The exposure band table is served by the reference data service (RWB-E1-043, LOV-EXPOSURE).
- The job runs as SY-CASEWF on the database identity `recovery_svc`, which holds SELECT on `recovery.v_ace_*` only for ACE data and no privilege on `public`. Readers hold Case record R (roles.txt permission matrix).

### 4. Flow

1. ACE: the ALS batch for business date 2026-12-01 completes. `v_ace_batch_status` holds the row with source ALS, status "COMPLETED" and `is_complete` true.
2. RWB-E1-015: raises AceDataReady (EV-26) for source ALS and business date 2026-12-01.
3. CaseCreationJob (JOB-02): starts on AceDataReady and reads the entry threshold parameter `case.entry.dpd`.
4. CaseCreationJob: asks the P1 interface for every account in `v_ace_account` with `days_past_due` at or above 90 and `data_as_of` equal to the business date that has no open `case_account` row (`linked_to` is null).
5. CaseCreationJob: for each account reads the BORROWER `person_key` from `v_ace_ca_relation` and the customer row from `v_ace_customer`. An account whose borrower key has no `v_ace_customer` row gets no case and is listed for REC-05.
6. EntryRuleEvaluator (SY-RULES): evaluates the exclusion rows in force for each account over the view flags and the `party_ext` and `account_ext` flags. An excluded account gets a `case_entry_exclusion` row with the `account_key`, reason code, rule ID and version, and no case.
7. CaseGrouping: groups the remaining accounts by the borrower `person_key` and the product group mapped from `v_ace_account.product_category` through LOV-PRODUCT. An existing open case for the same pair receives a new `case_account` row instead of a new case.
8. CaseNumberIssuer: takes the next number from the sequence for the entry year and formats RW-2026-000123.
9. CaseCreationService: in one transaction writes `recovery_case` with status NEW, `primary_person_key`, `dpd_at_entry`, `total_outstanding_at_entry` and the exposure band. It writes the `case_account` rows with `account_key` and `is_primary`, the BORROWER `case_party` row with `person_key`, and CaseCreated (EV-01) in the outbox.
10. CaseCreationService: as the last step before commit, calls DecisionLogWriter.write with decision code "D6", final call "CASE_CREATED", the entry rule version, the ALS business date and the inputs. A failed write rolls the transaction back.
11. CaseCreationJob: records counts (accounts read, excluded, linked, created, failed, no customer row) on the job record; JOB-03 starts after it.
12. Case owner: opens the case through GET /cases/{caseId} and its accounts through GET /cases/{caseId}/accounts.

### 8. Business rules

| ID | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BR1 | An account enters recovery when `days_past_due` in `v_ace_account` for the ACE business date is at or above the entry threshold | Parameter case.entry.dpd = 90; DL16; DF-ACE-018 |
| BR2 | One case per borrower `person_key` and product group, with every qualifying account linked by `account_key` | DL17; REQ-CWC-003 |
| BR3 | An account matching an exclusion row gets no case; the reason, rule ID and version are recorded in `case_entry_exclusion` | Exclusion rows (rule table EXCLUSION); REQ-CWC-004 |
| BR4 | The case number is RW, the entry year and a six-digit number from a yearly sequence; it is issued once and never reused | recovery_case.case_no (DF-CAS-002); uk_recovery_case |
| BR5 | A new case has status NEW and no owner until allocation | casemodel.txt Case status "New" |
| BR6 | Status values are read from the list, not from code | LOV-CASE-STATUS; Principle 1 |
| BR7 | No case is created before the ALS batch for the business date is COMPLETED in `v_ace_batch_status`. A late or failed batch raises AceDataLate (EV-27), and the case is created on the next complete batch | CTL-02; JOB-02 "No cases from stale data"; RWB-E1-015 |
| BR8 | Every case creation writes its decision log entry in the same transaction; a failed write rolls the creation back | Principle 5; R-UNI-39 |
| BR9 | Exposure band is set from the total outstanding at entry using the band table in force | recovery_case.exposure_band (DF-CAS-015); LOV-EXPOSURE |
| BR10 | A case created on bad data is canceled by rule, never deleted | R-UNI-16 (Epic 2 handling) |
| BR11 | The case links to ACE by business key (`recovery_case.primary_person_key`, `case_account.account_key`, `case_party.person_key`) with no foreign key into ACE. A key with no view row is listed by REC-05 and never filled from a partial record | D-RCV-35 U4; ADR-010; Change Register Appendix B; REC-05 |
| BR12 | The primary account is the linked account with the highest `total_balance` on the business date; a tie breaks on the lowest `account_key` | case_account.is_primary (DF-CAS-027); to be confirmed by the product owner |

### 13. Solution plan

**Package.** `recovery/casewf/casecreation`, Java package `recovery.casewf.casecreation`.

**Components**

| Component | Type | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| CaseCreationJob | Job | JOB-02; listens for AceDataReady for ALS; checks the ALS batch row for the business date; runs the entry rule per `account_key`; writes job counts |
| EntryRuleEvaluator | Service | Applies the threshold and the exclusion rows over the view and extension flags; returns ENTER or EXCLUDE with reason and rule version |
| CaseGrouping | Service | Groups accounts by the BORROWER `person_key` and product group; finds an open case to link to; applies the primary account rule |
| CaseNumberIssuer | Service | Draws from sequence case_no_seq_YYYY and formats RW-YYYY-NNNNNN; refuses above 999999 |
| CaseCreationService | Service | Case, account links, party link and outbox event, then the decision log write, in one transaction |
| CaseAndWorkflowRepository | Repository | recovery_case, case_account, case_party, case_entry_exclusion |
| CaseAndWorkflowController | Controller | GET /cases/{caseId}, GET /cases/{caseId}/accounts, caseNo search on GET /cases |
| CaseStatusCatalog | Service | Reads LOV-CASE-STATUS through the P4 list interface; no Java enum of statuses |
| ExclusionRuleTable | Rule table | Rule rows with rule table EXCLUSION, read through RuleTableReader (P4) |

**Database**

- Writes recovery_case (all columns), case_account (all columns), case_party (all columns), case_entry_exclusion, outbox.
- Reads ACE data only through `v_ace_account`, `v_ace_customer`, `v_ace_ca_relation` and `v_ace_batch_status` (through P1). Reads `party_ext` and `account_ext` by business key. No copy of an ACE row is written.
- No foreign key from `recovery_case.primary_person_key`, `case_account.account_key`, `case_party.person_key` or `case_entry_exclusion.account_key` into ACE (Appendix B). Integrity is checked by REC-05.
- Constraint: partial unique index `ux_case_open_customer_product ON recovery_case (primary_person_key, product_group) WHERE closed_date IS NULL`.
- Constraint: partial unique index `ux_case_account_open ON case_account (account_key) WHERE linked_to IS NULL`, so an account is on one open case.
- Constraint from V0001: `uk_case_entry_exclusion UNIQUE (business_date, account_key)`, one exclusion row per account and business date.
- Migration in this story: `V018_1__case_creation.sql` creates the yearly sequences case_no_seq_2026 and case_no_seq_2027 (created ahead by the job each December) and the two partial unique indexes above. The tables come from V0001 (RWB-E1-001) and the views from V0002 and V0003 (RWB-E1-069). No column is added for source data.

### 17. Guardrails

- No business value in code: the threshold, the exclusion reasons, the bands and the status list are data.
- No personal data in logs, tests, events or prompts. JOB-02 reads business keys and flags only: no name, address or identity number. Test data uses synthetic account and customer keys only.
- No read of another module's tables. ACE data only through recovery.v_ace_* views: account, customer, relation and batch data come through RecoveryDataQuery (P1) over `v_ace_account`, `v_ace_customer`, `v_ace_ca_relation` and `v_ace_batch_status`; rules through RuleTableReader (P4).
- The case links to ACE by business key with no foreign key into ACE; a key with no view row is listed by REC-05 and nothing is filled from a partial record.
- The case number is printed on every outbound document; other modules take it from CaseRecordService, never from a copy.
