### 1. Story

As a **data steward and data product owner (RW-DATASTEW)**, I want **every data product to carry the business date, start time and completion time of the ACE batch it came from, exposed to every consumer and shown on the case record view, and case intake to run only when the ACE batch of each source it needs is complete for the business date**, so that **every decision input states how old it is, stale facts are visible, and no case is built from a half-loaded day**.

### 2. Business rationale

The Workbench loads no source data; ACE does (D-RCV-35, U1). ACE writes one `ace_batch_run` row per source system and business date (RWB-ACE-001), and the Workbench reads it through `recovery.v_ace_batch_status`. A fact on the case can be one day or one week old, and nobody can see which. A stamp per product and business date, shown on the case record and copied into each decision's inputs snapshot, makes the age of every input visible to the person deciding and to audit. A product whose ACE batch has not completed by its expected-by time is flagged stale the same morning, and the previous ACE data stays in use. The same reading is the daily completeness gate that the removed data quality story (RWB-E1-006) used to hold: case intake for a business date waits until every source it needs is complete. Serves REQ-RDF-009; measure: the ACE batch is complete when the Workbench reads it on 99 percent of days (FSD P1 A2.14.1).

### 3. Preconditions

- RWB-E1-001 is done: V0001 holds `data_product_load` and `data_product`.
- RWB-E1-064 is done: the data_product table holds DP01 to DP14 with refresh cycle and expected-by time (08:00 for daily products and MON 08:00 for weekly products; DL11).
- RWB-E1-069 is done: the recovery module reads ACE only through `recovery.v_ace_*` views. `v_ace_batch_status` is a Section B view (V0003).
- RWB-ACE-001 is done: ACE writes one `ace_batch_run` row per source system and business date. Until then the feature stays behind its flag (DL43).
- The product-to-source map (section 12) is loaded as a reference table through RWB-E1-043.
- RWB-E1-027 is done: the P7 decision log writer builds an inputs snapshot for each entry.
- The recovery service identity (recovery_svc) holds SELECT on `recovery.v_ace_*` and no privilege on `public`.
- The reader holds Case record R (case roles) or Platform administration R (data steward, platform administrator).

### 4. Flow

1. BatchStatusListener: reads `v_ace_batch_status` for a new or changed row, one row per source system and business date.
2. FreshnessStampService: for a COMPLETED row, reads the product-to-source map and, in one transaction, writes one data_product_load row for each product the source feeds, with business date D, `source_extracted_at` from `started_at`, `loaded_at` from `completed_at` and status LOADED. It raises EV-26 AceDataReady for the source and business date.
3. FreshnessStampService: for a product fed by more than one source, sets the stamp to the oldest business date among its sources.
4. DailyCompletenessGate: before JOB-02 case intake runs for business date D, reads `v_ace_batch_status` for each source that intake needs (ALS for DP01 and SCBPDM for DP02, from the map). When every one is COMPLETED for D, it returns COMPLETE and JOB-02 starts.
5. DailyCompletenessGate: when a needed source is not COMPLETED for D, it returns HOLD and names the product and the source. JOB-02 does not start for D. EV-27 AceDataLate is published for that source, the product is marked STALE and the alert goes out with MSG-P1-001.
6. FreshnessCheckStep: at each product's expected-by time, checks that a COMPLETED row exists in `v_ace_batch_status` for the expected business date of each of its sources.
7. FreshnessStampService: for a product with no COMPLETED row, marks the stamp in use (the last complete business date) STALE with `stale_flagged_at`, publishes EV-27 AceDataLate and sends the operations alert. No row is written for a business date with no complete batch.
8. BatchStatusListener: on a FAILED row, marks the product STALE at once and publishes EV-27. The stamp for the last complete business date stays the one in use.
9. Consumer (case record view, P3 or P7): calls FreshnessQuery or GET /data-products/freshness and receives business date, source start time, completion time and status per product.
10. Case record view: shows the stamp in the data panel, with MSG-P1-001 when the product is stale.
11. P7 inputs snapshot builder: calls FreshnessQuery.stampsFor() for the products a decision reads and writes each stamp into the snapshot that inputs_ref points to.
12. BatchStatusListener: a later COMPLETED row for D sets status LOADED, clears the stale flag, raises EV-26, and the gate releases JOB-02 for D.

| ID | Trigger | What happens | Message | Covered by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| X1 | No COMPLETED batch for the expected business date by the expected-by time | Product set STALE; EV-27 published; operations alert sent; case record shows the stale warning; the previous ACE data stays in use | MSG-P1-001 | AC2 |
| X2 | The ACE batch row is FAILED | Product set STALE at once; EV-27 published; the previous stamp stays in use; case intake does not start | MSG-P1-001 | AC4 |

| X4 | A late batch completes after the stale flag | Status LOADED with the new completion time; stale flag cleared; EV-26 raised | None | AC6 |
| X5 | A batch completes one minute before the expected-by time | Product not flagged stale | None | AC7 |
| X6 | A weekly product inside its weekly cycle | Product not flagged stale on the days between batches | None | AC8 |

### 8. Business rules

| ID | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BR1 | Every COMPLETED ACE batch row writes one freshness stamp per product it feeds and business date, read from `v_ace_batch_status` only | REQ-RDF-009; BR-P1-014-2 |
| BR2 | A product with no COMPLETED batch for its expected business date by its expected-by time is stale | data_product.expected_by (RWB-E1-064); DL11 |
| BR3 | A late or failed ACE batch leaves the previous ACE data and the stamp of the last complete business date in use, and marks the product stale | VR-P1-014-1; EV-27 |
| BR4 | Every decision records the freshness of each data product it read | REQ-RDF-009; FEAT-DRI-12; BR-P1-014-5; DF-DEC-008 meaning |
| BR5 | A repeat read of the same COMPLETED batch row for the same product and business date is ignored and counted | R-UNI-40 |
| BR6 | Expected-by times, refresh cycles and the product-to-source map are data rows, not code | Principle 1; DT02; BR-P1-014 |
| BR7 | Case intake (JOB-02) for a business date does not start until the ACE batch of each source it needs is COMPLETED for that date. When one is not, intake is held, EV-27 AceDataLate is raised and the data steward is alerted. This replaces the daily gate of the removed RWB-E1-006 | BR-P1-014-4; CTL-02; Change Register Appendix A |
| BR8 | A product fed by more than one source carries the oldest business date among its sources and is stale while any of them is late | BR-P1-014-3 |
| BR9 | Freshness is never inferred from a view's data_as_of, a row count or a file | BR-P1-014-2 |

### 9. State transitions

None. This story does not change a case status. It changes the freshness status of a data product load: LOADED to STALE when the expected ACE batch is missing, late or failed, and STALE to LOADED when a later COMPLETED batch arrives. The gate result (COMPLETE or HOLD) is held in the JOB-02 job run, not in a table of its own.

### 13. Solution plan

- Package: folder `recovery/dataengine/freshness`, Java package `recovery.dataengine.freshness`.

| Component | Type | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| FreshnessStampService | Service | Writes stamps from the ACE batch row; marks and clears stale |
| BatchStatusListener | Listener | Reads new or changed rows in v_ace_batch_status; drives the stamp, EV-26 and EV-27 |
| FreshnessCheckStep | Job | Cut-off check per product at data_product.expected_by |
| DailyCompletenessGateImpl | Service | Implements DailyCompletenessGate over v_ace_batch_status and the product-to-source map |
| AceBatchStatusRepository | Repository | Read-only access to `recovery.v_ace_batch_status` |
| DataProductLoadRepository | Repository | Reads and writes data_product_load |
| DataProductLoad | Entity | One row per product and business date |
| FreshnessQuery | Published interface | Stamps for other modules |
| FreshnessController | Controller | GET /data-products/freshness |
| DataFreshnessPanel | React component | Data panel on SCR-03 with business date, load time and the stale warning |

- Database: no new table. data_product_load is created by V0001 (RWB-E1-001) with id uuid, product_code varchar(25), source_system varchar(40), business_date date, source_extracted_at timestamptz, loaded_at timestamptz, status varchar(10), stale_flagged_at timestamptz, duplicate_count integer default 0, plus the standard columns; unique (product_code, business_date). The index (product_code, loaded_at desc) ships in the next free migration. `v_ace_batch_status` is created by V0003 (RWB-E1-069, Section B) over `public.ace_batch_run` (RWB-ACE-001). Reads data_product (RWB-E1-064). Reads `v_ace_account.data_as_of` and `v_ace_payment.data_as_of` only to pick the stamp for a row.
- Configuration and reference data: data_product.refresh_cycle and expected_by, loaded by the data steward through RWB-E1-064; the product-to-source map through RWB-E1-043; properties `rwb.dataengine.freshness.alert-channel` and `rwb.dataengine.freshness.poll-interval` set by the platform administrator.
- Build steps:
  1. The index migration and DataProductLoadRepository with its unique key.
  2. AceBatchStatusRepository over `recovery.v_ace_batch_status`, read only.
  3. FreshnessStampService.recordBatch(sourceSystem, businessDate, startedAt, completedAt) called by BatchStatusListener; a repeat increments duplicate_count and raises no second EV-26.
  4. BatchStatusListener on a FAILED row: writes STALE at once and publishes EV-27, idempotent on source, business date and status.
  5. FreshnessCheckStep with one Quartz trigger per product built from data_product; writes STALE, publishes EV-27 and sends the alert.
  6. DailyCompletenessGateImpl over the map; JOB-02 (RWB-E1-018) calls it before its first step and stops on HOLD.
  7. FreshnessQuery and FreshnessController with DataProductFreshness responses.
  8. P7 inputs snapshot builder (RWB-E1-027) calls stampsFor() and writes each stamp into the snapshot.
  9. DataFreshnessPanel on SCR-03 with the Stale input state and MSG-P1-001.
  10. Tests for AC1 to AC11 with a fixed test clock and synthetic batch rows, and the access test.
- Feature flag: `rwb.dataengine.freshness-stamp`. It is on only after RWB-ACE-001 creates `ace_batch_run` and V0003 creates `v_ace_batch_status` (DL43). Before that the panel shows no stamp rather than a guessed one.
- Done when: the tests in section 16 pass and JOB-02 calls DailyCompletenessGate before it starts.

### 17. Guardrails

- No business value in code: expected-by times, cycles, the product-to-source map, the alert channel and the poll interval are data rows or properties.

- The stale check and the gate never delete or change ACE data or a stamp already written; the previous ACE data stays in use.
