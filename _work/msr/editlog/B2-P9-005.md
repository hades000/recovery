## Design decisions recorded in the stories

The case-side recompute of the segment, the route reopen and the hard-gate re-run of R-UNI-22 and R-UNI-37 stay with rwb-case (GAP-2586; ASM-4281).

The rule rows and the rule version reach the service on the rwb-decision read of `/api/recovery/decision/v1`, declared and faked with no C row, because the work package gives this key no contract dependency; the member names and the upstream reclassification event name carry the placeholder (GAP-2585; ASM-4277).

5. A day that is PUBLISHED or frozen is never rewritten; a late change is written for the next unpublished business date and the record names both dates (BR5 of the APP story).
