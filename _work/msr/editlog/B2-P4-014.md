## The design decision this WP had to make

Legacy RWB-E8-014 is a stub in every source: one story sentence, two generic criteria, four test cases, an endpoints table that belongs to the rule-version keys (`POST /admin/rule-versions` and its approve), a data table of `assignment`, `decision_log_entry`, `rule_row` and `role`, and screens SCR-39 and SCR-41. FSD P4 v3 A2.25 adds an intent, three generic steps, VR-P4-025-1 (an ACE data-product freshness rule) and BR-P4-025; TSD P4 v3 has no such feature and its appendix T-A declares no pack operation; the context pack declares no P4 event and four REST operations, none of them a pack operation.

The slice was therefore built on the only reading the sources support for this service: a **pack** is a candidate version of this service's own access model (an ordered set of `PERMISSION`, `SCOPE` and `MASKING` rows), the **check** compares it with `role`, `permission` and `masking_rule` in force, and a pack that fails is **returned** to its `ownerRole` with one defect per failing row, recorded against the pack version and the rule version in force. The three checks map one-to-one on the requirements the key serves: PC-ENT on REQ-ACA-003 and REQ-ACA-007, PC-SCP on REQ-ACA-009 and PC-MSK on REQ-ACA-005. Nothing is applied by the check, so the load and the approval of a matrix version stay with RWB-P4-AD002-APP, AD010-APP and AD013-APP, the scope table with AD003-APP, the masking load with AD004-APP, the sign-in conflict check with AD005-APP and the delegation table with AD016-APP.

Every invented element is carried as a candidate gap or assumption and is cited from the text; no business value is written in code, and the personal areas, the row limit, the page bounds and the label shape are parameter rows behind DP-06.
