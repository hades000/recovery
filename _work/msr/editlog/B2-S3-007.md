## Notes and residual risks

- The balance after the guaranteed share is composed, not stored: it is the balance read at the ACE batch date less the share the answered row names. This is the working read of ASM-4537 and no column is added; if a field is allocated, the activity reads it instead and the element keeps its shape.
- The close of the guarantee has no claim status: `LOV-CLAIM-STATUS` stops at `PAID` and `REJECTED`. The two stories record the closing basis in the element and leave the case closure to rwb-case at `GUARANTEE_CLOSED`, and the gap stays open rather than adding a reference value on their own authority.
- The subrogation position is carried by the element; the state and its entry rules (R-GUA-23, R-GUA-24) belong to RWB-S3-E4016-APP, and a position record of its own would need its identifiers published first.
- The step is entered on the case signal the parent forwards. No timer, no scheduled scan and no subscription is invented, and no event is published, because the ownership rows give S3 no event and the plan gives this legacy key no DP-09 dependency.
- The three ACE views are read through this service's login under DP-05, which conflicts with Architecture v5 slide 8; the grant is the working read of ASM-4541 and the conflict is left to the architecture owner rather than resolved here.
- The `-ITG` copies of TC-E4-019-01 to -06 belong to the wave 3 integration harness (RWB-ITG-W3) and are not written by this WP, so the two stories carry the legacy case IDs without the suffix.
- The story files exceed the 20-25 KB aim of WP item 10 (48 KB and 49 KB), as the sibling story files of the other services in this stage do: the metadata table, the four legacy criteria, the rule tables, the §12 tables and the test-case tables are kept complete and exact, and no non-table section exceeds 30 lines except the Gherkin block of §7.
