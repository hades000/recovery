# 1. What is sent

| Kind | Examples | Channel | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Statutory notice | Demand, recall, statutory sale notice, guarantor notice | Registered mail through WIN | Proof of delivery from WIN |
| Customer letter | Offer, settlement terms, restructure terms | Registered or ordinary mail; e-signature link | Delivery record; signed copy |
| Message | Reminder, offer ending, payment received | SMS through NSS; LINE OA | Delivery receipt from the channel |
| Partner instruction | Instruction, recall, fee approval | Partner portal | Acceptance by the partner |
| Internal alert | Task, warning, escalation | Workbench and email | Read on the case timeline |

The five kinds and their channels are unchanged. In the target they are carried by three services, not by one module, and the table below says which one. A notice is a row of `rwb_comms` (P6 rwb-comms); the letter file attached to it is a document of `rwb_document` (P5); the file that carries it to WIN is delivered by `rwb_integration` (P2); the message leaves through ACE Common (Architecture v5 slides 17 and 30; Ownership Map v1).

## 1.1 Target channels and owners

| Kind | Producer | How it leaves | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| Statutory notice | P6 rwb-comms, notice type DEMAND, RECALL, STATUTORY_SALE_NOTICE or GUARANTOR_NOTICE | WIN registered mail: INT-14 daily outbound file, written by P6 at the 16:00 window (JOB-10) and delivered by P2 rwb-integration | `rwb_comms.notice`; the proof of delivery arrives on INT-15 (ASM-B-TECH-14-6; ASM-B-TECH-14-12) |

# 2. How a notice is produced

- A rule raises the notice (R-LIT-03 for demand and recall).

- The Workbench checks the contact stop and the consented channel. A stop holds the notice (R-LIT-05).

- It fills the current template version approved by Legal for the type, product and language.

- The litigation officer approves the notice where the template requires approval.

- The notice goes to WIN in the daily outbound file through the outbox.

- WIN returns the barcode, then the proof of delivery in the weekly file.

- The delivery date starts the notice period (R-LIT-07). No proof within the window marks the notice unserved (R-LIT-08).

## 2.1 The same seven steps in the target

| Step | Owner | Call type | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. A rule raises the notice | S4 rwb-litigation for demand and recall (R-LIT-03); any route service | Temporal child NoticeDispatchWorkflow on queue rwb-comms, or REST POST /cases/{caseId}/notices for a staff request | The request names the case, the notice type and the template code; the kit template of the workflow is the one Architecture v5 slide 17 names |
| 2. Contact stop and consented channel | P6 rwb-comms, with S8 rwb-deviation and P1 rwb-source-data | Kafka EV-06 DeviationRaised and EV-07 DeviationCleared; the P1 contract for the contact point | R-LIT-05 holds the notice when the stop applies; R-UNI-10 keeps the statutory notice when consent is withdrawn (section 4) |
| 3. Template selection | P6 rwb-comms | The service's own tables | The current version of `rwb_comms.template` for the type, product group and language (section 3) |
| 4. Fill the template and generate the letter | P6 rwb-comms, with P5 rwb-document | REST POST /cases/{caseId}/generated-documents of P5; one hop, 2 s timeout | Merge values come from the `rwb_comms.merge_field` catalog only; the generated document ID is kept on the notice |
| 5. Approval where the template requires it | P7 rwb-decision | Temporal child ApprovalWorkflow of P7; POST /notices/{noticeId}/approve of S4 for the litigation officer's decision | The notice waits in Approved until the approval is decided; a refused approval leaves it out of the file (CG-B-TECH-14-7; ASM-B-TECH-14-9) |
| 6. Dispatch | P6 rwb-comms, with P2 rwb-integration | Kafka EV-13 NoticeGenerated and EV-14 NoticeDispatched; Temporal deliverFile of P2; file INT-14 | The notice, its local decision record and the outbox row are one transaction (R-UNI-39); JOB-10 writes the daily file at 16:00 and P2 delivers it |
| 7. Barcode and proof of delivery | P1 rwb-source-data reads WIN; P6 rwb-comms records it | ACE ingestion INT-15 and the view `v_ace_pod`, read by P1; Kafka EV-15 NoticeDelivered | The barcode, the callback date and the delivery status are written on the notice row (CG-B-TECH-14-4; ASM-B-TECH-14-6; ASM-B-TECH-14-12) |
| 8. Notice period | S4 rwb-litigation | Temporal timer on the case workflow | The delivery date starts the notice period (R-LIT-07); no proof inside the 7-day proof window marks the notice unserved (R-LIT-08) |

No step of the seven is dropped. Steps 1, 3, 4 and 8 stay where they are today in the business sense; steps 2, 6 and 7 now cross a service boundary, and the crossing is a contract or an event, never a read of another service's table (DP-01).

# 3. Templates

| Item | Design |
|---|---|
| Held as | Template rows with code, version, language, product group, body and Legal approval |
| Merge fields | From a catalog held as data; a template may use only catalog fields |
| Approval | Legal counsel approves each version; Compliance approves customer wording |
| Withdrawal | A withdrawn template holds generation (R-LIT-04) |
| Language | Thai by default; English where the customer chose English (DL10) |

## 3.1 Where the templates live in the target

- The template is a row of `rwb_comms.template`, owned by P6 rwb-comms (P6). It carries the code, the version, the language, the product group, the body reference, the Legal approval flag, the valid-from date, the channel, the notice type, the signature basis and the merge codes (Architecture v5 slide 41; TSD P6 T3.1; Ownership Map v1).

- The merge field catalog is `rwb_comms.merge_field`, owned by P6, versioned by catalog version; a template may use only a code the catalog holds. No merge value is written in code (Architecture v5 slide 41).

- A notice keeps the template version it was generated from, so a later version does not change an issued notice.

- The template screens are the P6 endpoints of the API specification: GET /admin/templates, POST /admin/templates, GET /admin/templates/{templateId}/content, POST /admin/templates/{templateId}/approve and POST /admin/templates/{templateId}/withdraw (Ownership Map v1, Endpoints).

- Legal approval is recorded on the template version with its valid-from date. The Compliance approval of customer wording has no recorded artefact in the target (CG-B-TECH-14-12; ASM-B-TECH-14-14).

- Version 3 reads the ownership of the template and its fields from Architecture v5 slide 41 and the Ownership Map v1, which give both to P6 rwb-comms. The Decision Log v10 answer to DL28, recorded on 13 November 2026, gives the templates and the fields to the document module (P5). The conflict is raised in section 6 and recorded as a gap (CG-B-TECH-14-3; ASM-B-TECH-14-5).

# 4. Contact rules

- No message goes to a channel without consent; statutory notices continue when consent is withdrawn (R-UNI-10).

- A contact stop for a complaint, death or legal hold stops messages and letters except statutory notices where the law requires them.

- Contact times and frequency follow the Debt Collection Act and bank policy, held as reference data.

- Every message sent is written to the interaction ledger.

## 4.1 Where each rule is enforced in the target

| Rule | Where it is enforced | Source of the value |
|---|---|---|
| Consent per channel | A step of NoticeDispatchWorkflow in P6 rwb-comms, before any send or any file line | The contact point and its consent state are held by P1 rwb-source-data in `contact_point_ext` and read through the P1 contract; the P6 screen maintains them through that contract (CG-B-TECH-14-5; ASM-B-TECH-14-7) |
| Contact stop | P6 rwb-comms on EV-06 DeviationRaised and EV-07 DeviationCleared from S8 rwb-deviation; the notice is held with a hold reason that names the stop | S8 rwb-deviation owns the deviation and the stop; the codes are X1 to X3 per the Temporal and Drools design, while R-LIT-05 names X1 and X2 (CG-B-TECH-14-6; ASM-B-TECH-14-8) |
| Contact times and frequency | Read at the send step; no value in code | Reference data of P3 rwb-case through the reference-data contract (DP-07; CG-B-TECH-14-11; ASM-B-TECH-14-13) |
| Interaction ledger | P6 rwb-comms writes the entry through the P1 contract in the same step as the send | P1 rwb-source-data is the ledger owner of record (DP-18); POST /cases/{caseId}/interactions; a failed ledger write leaves the message unsent (ASM-B-TECH-14-17) |

The statutory notice is not held by a withdrawn consent, because R-UNI-10 keeps it; it is held by a contact stop only where the law does not require service (section 1 and the row above).

## 7.3 Guardrails

- No business value in code: notice types, hold reasons, delivery statuses, channels, contact times and frequency are rows with a version (Architecture v5 slide 47; sections 3 and 4).

- No personal data in events, workflow inputs, workflow history or logs. A notice event carries the notice ID, the case ID and the notice type (ADR-015; ADR-016; ADR-023).

- ACE data only through the owner's contract or the read-only `v_ace_*` views. The contact point and the proof of delivery are read through P1 rwb-source-data; no service reads `rwb_source_data` or `recovery.v_ace_pod` directly (DP-01; DP-05; ADR-010).

- One hop and a 2 s timeout on every REST call of this design (ADR-020).
