## 3
What we are building
The Recovery Workbench is the system that owns every recovery case from 90 days past due to close, for Mortgage, Hire Purchase and SSME. It is built as the recovery module of ACE, the bank's collection engine, so collection and recovery work in one application with one case view.
From day 90 to close
One case per customer and product, with one owner, one clock and one history
Mortgage, Hire Purchase, SSME
Every route: restructure, settlement, deed in lieu, guarantee claim, litigation, asset disposal, debt sale and write-off
Built on ACE
One service, one repository and one front end, with data through COLLINS

## 7
How the work runs
Rule
1
No more than three planning or review meetings a week for a squad member, and none longer than 30 minutes
2
The sponsor review and the steering committee look back at finished work and are outside that limit
3
Work flows on a Kanban board with limits on work in progress per column and a written pull policy
4
The monthly release is the only date the business sees. What slips moves to the next epic; the date does not move
5
Planning happens daily at the board. There is no sprint commitment and no velocity target

What happens
1 Raise
Anyone raises a question the build cannot answer. The product owner adds it to the decision log with an ID
2 Frame
The analyst writes the question, the options, a recommendation, what it blocks and what happens if it is late

The product owner names one owner and a date needed. Decision owners are bank roles
