[p] 6. Parallel run
[p] Each team runs its spreadsheet and the Workbench side by side for two weeks after its first release. The data steward reconciles daily. The spreadsheet retires when the business lead signs ten clean days.
[p] In the target the parallel run is run per schema, because each schema is released with its own service: a team's spreadsheet retires against the schema that replaces it, two weeks after that schema's release (section 10.2). The ten clean days, the daily reconciliation and the business-lead sign-off are unchanged.
[p] 7. Reconciliation and the cut-over check
[p] Counts and balances per data product reconcile to the source within the tolerance under DL25. Breaks are listed for the data steward and cleared before cutover.
[p] The cut-over check is the case list. Every case_list_row.account_key must resolve to a row in v_ace_account for the cut-over business date, and the count of open cases by product and bucket must agree with the view. A key that does not resolve is either a closed account or a key error; both are cleared before go-live, and the result is recorded against reconciliation rule REC-05. No case starts on the Workbench until this check passes.
[p] Under D-RCV-60 the case-list check stays the single global gate and keeps its owner: case_list_row is P1's daily published projection and the case list screen and API stay with P3. Section 10.1 adds the per-schema count and balance check that runs before each schema's own cutover.

[p] 10. Cut-over, reconciliation and parallel run per schema
[p] 10.1 Reconciliation per schema
[p] Each schema reconciles its own counts and balances to its source within the tolerance under DL25 before its cutover, and the data steward clears its breaks. Every schema records its result as a row in reconciliation_result (P1 rwb-source-data), one row per schema and business date, so the per-schema results and the global case-list result of section 7 sit together.
[p] A schema whose owner has no migrated table starts empty on its own baseline and records a zero-row result; it has no break to clear and no spreadsheet to retire.
[p] 10.2 Parallel run per schema
[p] Each team runs its spreadsheet beside the schema that replaces it for two weeks after that schema's release. The data steward reconciles daily per schema, and the spreadsheet retires when the business lead signs ten clean days for that schema. A team whose work spans several schemas retires each list against the schema that now holds it, not against the last service to go live.
[p] 10.3 Gate on every schema cutover
[p] No schema accepts writes before RWB-PLT-NF06-DB has created it and RWB-PLT-N011-DB has granted its login; no schema goes live before its own Flyway sequence has run and its reconciliation row is signed; and no case starts on the Workbench before the case-list check of section 7 passes, as section 7 already requires. The legacy tables stay read-only in place after their schema cuts over, for the retention period of each table (CG-B-DATA-21-7; ASM-B-DATA-21-7).
