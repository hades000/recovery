60:### 3.1 Context
61:
62:The Workbench is the recovery module of ACE. It takes every case from 90 days past due, for Mortgage, Hire Purchase and SSME, and carries decisions D6 to D9 with their sub-decisions, D5 once the restructure program is live, and deviations X1 to X7 after day 90. Epics 1 and 2 put the case list, the case layer, the owner, the timer and the approval path in place; Epics 3 to 8 are where the recovery money is actually made and lost.
63:
64:Source data arrives through ACE ingestion and is read only through the read-only `recovery.v_ace_*` views (D-RCV-35, ADR-010). The Workbench loads no source data and holds only the case, its decisions, reference data and four extension tables. From Epic 3 the case lifecycle runs on Temporal, one workflow per case, and every rule is a versioned data row evaluated by embedded Drools inside a workflow step (D-RCV-36, ADR-011, ADR-012). Under D-RCV-60 the module is delivered as 19 services inside the ACE platform, each with its own schema, contract, topic and task queue (Architecture v5, ADR-013 to ADR-023).
