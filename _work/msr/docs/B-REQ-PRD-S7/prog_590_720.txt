656:# 10. Delivery model
657:
658:Under D-RCV-60 the program is delivered as 19 service epics, seven platform epics and five integration epics, and every story also carries its business epic EP01 to EP08 as a tag (U1, DL45). The eight business epics keep the releases in section 8. The requirement IDs in section 5, their text, their priorities, their business owners and their epic column do not change; what this section adds is which epic delivers each requirement, in which wave, and in what order the work can start.

714:## 10.4 Contract-first delivery
715:
716:Every service publishes its contract before its build starts, and no service waits for another service to be built (ADR-020).
717:
718:- The contract of a service is four things: its REST resources under /api/recovery/<short>/v1 behind gateway-bff, its topic rwb.<short>.events.v1 with its event names, its Temporal task queue rwb-<short> in the namespace rwb-recovery, and the call types it offers and accepts (REST, Kafka, Temporal, file).
719:- A contract story is Day 1 in every service epic and has no build dependency. Application stories build against the published contract and its fake, so a dependency on another service's contract never blocks a build.
