480:## 6.3 ACE ingestion and freshness
481:
482:The Workbench runs no inbound load (D-RCV-35, U1). Work that depends on source data starts when ACE records the batch for that source and business date as complete in v_ace_batch_status: case creation and the case list on the ALS batch, hand-over on the EFS batch. A late batch delays the work and raises an alert; it never starts a Workbench load. NFR-016, NFR-017 and NFR-019 are Workbench document and scheduling jobs; they load no source data.
