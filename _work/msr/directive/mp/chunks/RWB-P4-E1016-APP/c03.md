## 1. GAP-0481 (Register gap; ruling R-21962; sections: find it)
- Title: The masking rules stay in P1 in the TSD while the Ownership Map rules the table to rwb-access (P4)
- Detail: The Technical Specification keeps masking_rule in P1's schema and describes P1 applying the mask itself, while the Ownership Map gives masking_rule, role and permission to rwb-access (P4) and Architecture v5 slides 14 and 41 give P4 the masking policy. Read as written, the story would hold a copy of another service's table and read it by SQL, which the ownership rule, the per-service login rule and boundary rule BND-6 all forbid.
- Evidence: TSD P1 v3 T3.6 'masking_rule is created by V0001 and its content is owned by RWB-E1-037'; Ownership Map v1 tables.masking_rule owner P4; Architecture v5 slide 14 P4 OUT 'masking policy' and slide 41 P4 'Recovery roles as data, scope, masking policy'
- Proposed fix: P1 holds data_product_access only. The masking rows, the role list and the permission matrix stay with rwb-access, and P1 takes the answer from POST /api/recovery/access/v1/authorise and the FieldMasker interface of RWB-P4-E1037-CON, one hop with a 2 s timeout, and names no masking, role or permission table in a query.
- Owner role: Solution architect
