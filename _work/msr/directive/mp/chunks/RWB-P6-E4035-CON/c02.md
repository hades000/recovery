- Evidence: Architecture v5 slide 42 row 15 NoticeDelivered with consumers rwb-litigation, rwb-guarantee and rwb-case; TSD P6 v2 T5.3 lists NoticeDelivered as published with no trigger; RWB P6 E1055 APP and E2021 APP publish it for the WIN path

State the once-per-notice rule in the Kafka Topic and Event Catalog when it is next versioned.
