## 9. GAP-0838 (Register gap; ruling R-23040; sections: find it)
- Title: Who raises the incident behind a NOT_COVERED answer is not specified
- Detail: The FSD says the lookup returns NOT_COVERED and that an incident is raised to the RW-RISK queue with MSG-P7-010. MSG-P7-010 is a decision-centre message and the incident queue is not the access service's, and no source says whether the lookup call itself raises the incident, or the calling router does, or rwb-decision does on the routing request. The answer shape therefore cannot be fixed without a ruling on the owner of the incident.
- Evidence: FSD P4 v3 A2.6 AC-EXC-P4-006-04; legacy RWB-AD-016 AC2; RWB-P4-E1038-APP exception path X6; Architecture v5 Text Extract v1 slide 14 (rwb-decision owns approvals and the central ledger)
- Proposed fix: The lookup answers NOT_COVERED with MSG-P7-010 naming the decision and the product and an incidentReference to the RW-RISK queue; the incident record itself is raised on the routing side by rwb-decision, which owns MSG-P7-010 and the approval record, and this service produces the answer and the reference only.
- Owner role: Solution architect
