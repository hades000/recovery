- Detail: Slide 29 states that the login owns its schema and that each service runs its own Flyway migrations, which reads both as the owning login applying them and as a separate migrator applying them on the owner's behalf; no source names a migrator login. A build team cannot tell which identity its migrations run as, and a grant of DDL to the owning login would be either required or redundant depending on the answer.
- Evidence: Architecture v5 slide 29 rules text: 'each service has one database login that owns its schema and has no grant on any other schema ... - Proposed fix: State in RWB-PLT-N011-DB that the migrations are applied by a per-service migrator login granted on its own service's schema only, that the owning login keeps ownership and that this story grants no DDL beyond ownership.
