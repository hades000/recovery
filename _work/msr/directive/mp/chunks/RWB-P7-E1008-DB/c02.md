## 10. GAP-2074 (Register gap; ruling R-21896; sections: find it)
- Title: One chain or 19 chains plus the ledger chain
- Detail: The source has one hash-chained decision log checked by one daily job. The target has a hash chain in every service and a second chain in the central ledger, anchored daily to write-once storage. No source states the scope of each chain (per service, per case or global), what prev_hash holds for the first record of a service, or which chain an auditor treats as the chain of record when the two disagree.
- Evidence: Source section 4 (one chain, one daily job); Architecture v5 slide 28 ('hash-linked to the previous record', 'chain verification', 'daily anchor of the ledger hash to immutable (WORM) storage'); Temporal and Drools Design v1 (chain_seq, prev_hash, entry_hash per record).
- Proposed fix: Each service chains its own records in chain_seq order, the ledger chains its entries on ingest, and the daily WORM fingerprint covers the ledger chain; the chain of record is the ledger, with the local chain available for a replay.
- Owner role: Service owner P7 rwb-decision with the 19 service owners
