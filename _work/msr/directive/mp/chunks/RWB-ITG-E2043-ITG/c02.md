## 9. GAP-2016 (Register gap; ruling R-22047; sections: find it)
- Title: Timer row and durable timer kept in step by one activity
- Detail: Version 3 keeps every timer as a row in the timer table (E1-028) written through the TimerScheduler port, with JOB-05 as the five-minute check. Architecture v5 (slide 44, JOB-05) retires the check in favour of durable timers inside each workflow, and DP-10 moves the schedule into the owning service. No source states which of the two is authoritative when they disagree, or how a pause, a resume and a cancel are applied to both without a second write path.
- Evidence: Workflow Engine Design v3 section 4: 'Timers stay rows in the timer table (E1-028)' and 'JOB-05 checks due timers every five minutes and calls the port'. Architecture v5 slide 44, JOB-05: 'durable timers (retired)'. Architecture v5 slide 25: 'Timers that last for years'.
- Proposed fix: The durable timer is authoritative and the timer row is the audit copy, written by ApplyStateActivity in the same transaction as the state, and read back on a reopen.
- Owner role: Service owner P3 rwb-case with the platform team

## 10. GAP-2024 (Register gap; ruling R-22730; sections: find it)
- Title: Who pauses the stage clock when the stage runs as a child

Version 3 has one workflow, so the question did not arise. No source states whether the parent pauses a clock it does not hold, or instructs the child, and what happens to a child that is started while a deviation is open.
- Evidence: Architecture v5 slide 25 notes: 'DeviationWorkflow (rwb-deviation, can start at any time and pauses the stage clock)'. Temporal and Drools Design v1 slide 24 notes: 'the case router delivers DeviationRaised (EV-06) and DeviationCleared (EV-07) to the CaseWorkflow, which pauses and resumes the stage  ...
- Proposed fix: The parent forwards DeviationRaised to the running stage child, and the child pauses its own stage timer and resumes it on DeviationCleared, so the clock that is paused is the clock that is held.
- Owner role: Service owner P3 rwb-case with S8 rwb-deviation
