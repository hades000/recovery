- Owner role: ACE DB team with the platform team

## 2. GAP-1387 (Register gap; ruling R-29076; sections: find it)
- Title: Whether the service login or a separate migrator login applies the migrations is not specified
- Detail: Slide 29 states that the login owns its schema and that each service runs its own Flyway migrations, which reads both as the owning login applying them and as a separate migrator applying them on the owner's behalf; no source names a migrator login. The same Work Package carries a second story, RWB-PLT-N011-DB 'PgBouncer pools, migrator role and grant check', which implies a separate migrator without stating the split. A build team cannot tell which identity its migrations run as, and a grant of DDL to the owning login would be either required or redundant depending on the answer.
- Evidence: Architecture v5 slide 29 rules text: 'each service has one database login that owns its schema and has no grant on any other schema ... Each service runs its own Flyway migrations'; slide 39 decision 2 gives 'no cross-schema access' and no migrator; Template and Rules section R5.5 DP-15 gives the pe ...
- Proposed fix: State in RWB-PLT-N011-DB that the migrations are applied by a per-service migrator login granted on its own service's schema only, that the owning login keeps ownership and that this story grants no DDL beyond ownership.
- Owner role: Solution architect

## 3. GAP-0093 (Register gap; ruling R-29078; sections: find it)
- Title: RPO and RTO are not set in NFR Spec v3
- Detail: ADR-023 states the NFR v3 service targets, which include a screen response, a rule evaluation budget, a 60 second agent limit and 99.9% availability in business hours. RPO and RTO are not set, so the availability target cannot be tested against a recovery objective and the observability design has no recovery objective to report on.
- Evidence: Architecture v5 slide 48 'RPO/RTO: not yet set' and note 'NFR gap: RPO/RTO are not set in NFR Spec v3; proposed tiers'.
- Proposed fix: Set RPO and RTO per service tier in the next NFR Specification version and restate them in ADR-023.
- Owner role: Solution architect
