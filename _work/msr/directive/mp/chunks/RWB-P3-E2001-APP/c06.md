## 9. GAP-0589 (Register gap; ruling R-28698; sections: find it)
- Title: The history read is one operation or a paged read, and the HTTP answer of a held read, are stated by no source
- Detail: Two shape questions are open. (1) The legacy interface answers a complete List<EfsHistoryRecord> for one account and business date, and nothing states whether the target read answers the whole collection period in one call or pages it, nor what the row cap of one call is. (2) When the freshness gate refuses, no source states the HTTP status: the legacy job simply did not run, the P1 freshness gate answers COMPLETE or HOLD to a workflow, and the run's contract states no refusal status for a read that is held. A read that answers 200 with zero rows and a read that answers 409 with a problem body ...
- Evidence: Legacy RWB-E2-001 section 12 reads 'List<EfsHistoryRecord> EfsHistoryQuery.forAccount(String accountKey, LocalDate businessDate)' with no page argument; Architecture v5 slide 11 gives the REST rules as '2 s; retry GET only (once, jitter); breaker + bulkhead' and names no paging rule and no held stat ...
- Proposed fix: Answer one account and one business date in one call as the legacy interface did, with a configured row cap and no page parameter, and answer a held read with 409 and a problem body naming MSG-P1-001 and the date of the load in use, so the run can tell a hold from an empty history. Record both in RWB-P2-E2001-CON and in the API Specification at the next version.
- Owner role: Solution architect, P1
