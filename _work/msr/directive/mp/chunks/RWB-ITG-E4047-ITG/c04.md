## 2. GAP-2559 (Register gap; ruling R-28899; sections: find it)
- Title: The rejection of a posting is a route state for the debt sale and the write-off and has no stated owner for a settlement or a sale receipt
- Detail: R-DS-32 and R-WO-22 hold the posting and raise an exception task and move the debt-sale and write-off cases to POSTING_REJECTED; R-DS-33 and R-WO-23 re-post. No rule of the settlement or asset-sale route says what happens when the ledger rejects its posting, whether the case moves, and whether the receipt is re-posted from this service or from the interface's exception queue.
- Evidence: Routing logic v4: R-DS-32, R-DS-33 in the debt sale route set and R-WO-22, R-WO-23 in the write-off set; no POSTING_REJECTED row in the settlement or the asset disposal set
- Proposed fix: Keep the rejection as an exception item of the interface row with its retry and resend, and let the route service own any case state it wants to raise
- Owner role: Business analyst, P8
