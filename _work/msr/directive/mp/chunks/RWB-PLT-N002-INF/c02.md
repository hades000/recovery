## 1. GAP-0123 (Register gap; ruling R-23281; sections: find it)
- Title: No retry topic name is given for any event topic
- Detail: Architecture v5 fixes three retry windows, 1 min, 10 min and 1 h, and says that a record then goes to '.dlt', but it names no retry topic. RWB-PLT-N002-INF counts three retry topics per event topic, 60 in the set, and records the naming as an open point of the platform story. The topic catalogue can therefore give the count, the window of each retry topic and its owner, but not the name of a single retry topic.
- Evidence: Architecture v5 slide 9: 'retry 1 min, 10 min, 1 h then .dlt'. Architecture v5 slide 22 lists 'retry 1 min', 'retry 10 min', 'retry 1 h', '.dlt + alert'. RWB-PLT-N002-INF section 11 and section 12.9 state 60 retry topics and record the derived names as not specified in the source documents.
- Proposed fix: Agree the retry topic naming rule with the ACE platform team and write it into the topic catalogue: three retry topics per event topic, one per window, derived from the event topic name so that no service story has to invent a name.
- Owner role: Platform architect
