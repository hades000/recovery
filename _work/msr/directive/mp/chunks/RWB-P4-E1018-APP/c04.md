- Detail: The data dictionary keeps a cardinality row from decision_log_entry to recovery_case, which in a per-service schema can only hold across a schema boundary. Both facts change the storage of a required value, and the legacy key names neither.

- Proposed fix: Restate DF-DEC-002 as an indexed business key with no foreign key and drop the recovery_case row of T3.3, and state in T3.1 that the ALS business date is carried inside inputs_ref, so the ledger holds no case attribute and no cross-schema key.
