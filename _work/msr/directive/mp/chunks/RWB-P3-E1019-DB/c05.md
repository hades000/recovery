## 3. GAP-1177 (Register gap; ruling R-28519; sections: find it)
- Title: No source decides whether a reader keeps a local cache of the role and queue data
- Detail: DP-01 forbids reading another service's tables, and the architecture says permission data is cached from rwb-access, but no document says whether a consumer may keep a queue or role cache in its own schema, for how long it lives, or how it is invalidated when the owner publishes a new version. A cached queue row in rwb_comms would be a copy of P3 reference data and would serve a superseded version after a rollback, which is exactly what REQ-CWC-006 promises never to happen. The story decides against a cache table; that decision is the story's own and no source supports or forbids it.
- Evidence: Architecture v5 slide 14 notes; ownership.json holds no cache table for any service; RWB-P3-E1019-APP section 12.7 lists no cache and no consumer cache contract
- Proposed fix: State the caching rule for reference readers in the service template: a reader may cache for the life of one call only, and a longer-lived cache is a contract of the owner with a version and an invalidation event
- Owner role: Solution architect
