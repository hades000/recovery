## 1. GAP-2593 (Register gap; ruling R-28888; sections: find it)
- Title: No source names the event or the timer that starts the age check on a completed ACE batch, and S1 owns no job and no event
- Detail: FSD S1 A2.1.2 names three possible triggers of the step, 'a completed ACE batch, an event or a timer', and A2.1.4.4 declares no background event for the feature. The ownership rows give S1 no job and no event, so no Schedule and no subscription exists to start the check when a batch completes, and the only trigger a source supports is the route command that starts RouteDecisionWorkflow. The story runs the check as the first step of that workflow and builds no timer and no subscription.
- Evidence: FSD S1 A2.1.2 step 1 gives the trigger set as 'a completed ACE batch, an event or a timer'; FSD S1 A2.1.4.4 is the row 'None, No background event'; the ownership rows of the context pack give S1 no event and no job and one workflow, RouteDecisionWorkflow; TSD S1 T5.3 is the row 'None'.
- Proposed fix: If the business needs the check to run on batch completion, the data owner publishes a readiness event or the service owner adds a Temporal Schedule covering the business date; until then the check runs inside the route decision only.
- Owner role: S1 rwb-valuation service owner with the P1 rwb-source-data owner
