## 8. GAP-1620 (Register gap; ruling R-28680; sections: find it)

- Proposed fix: Record in ADR-017 that the registry accepts an XLS decision table and a DMN 1.6 model as two package formats on one runtime, that a package version names its format, and that the conversion is a version change with its own test evidence; keep both formats in the load checks and the model fetch.
