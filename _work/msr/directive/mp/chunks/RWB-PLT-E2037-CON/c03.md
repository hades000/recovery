## 8. GAP-2022 (Register gap; ruling R-23211; sections: find it)
- Title: Schedule ID, overlap policy, catch-up window and pause behaviour
- Detail: The job map gives each job a name, a time and a mechanism, and no more. No source gives the Schedule ID, the overlap policy for a run that is still open, the catch-up window after downtime, the pause-on-failure behaviour, or the retention of a Schedule's closed runs. - Evidence: Architecture v5 slide 44 columns: Job, Name, When, Owner, Mechanism. The Batch and Scheduling Design v4 gives times in Asia/Bangkok and no Schedule attribute. Workflow Engine Design v3 has no Schedules section.
