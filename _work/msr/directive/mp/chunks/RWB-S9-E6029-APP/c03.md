## 10. GAP-1838 (Register gap; ruling R-23653; sections: find it)
- Title: partner_instruction.case_id is a cross-schema foreign key to recovery_case, while ADR-014 requires a business key and no foreign key out of the schema
- Detail: The Logical Data Model types partner_instruction.case_id as CROSS_REF uuid with FK recovery_case, the relationship list shows partner_instruction to recovery_case as N:1 and mandatory, and the physical schema creates case_id uuid NOT NULL with no constraint to another schema. Architecture v5 slide 29 gives each service its own schema and login with no cross-schema access, and ADR-014 and DP-01 forbid a foreign key that leaves the schema. The FSD field catalog repeats partner_instruction.case_id with the meaning 'The recovery_case this record belongs to' in nine features.
- Evidence: LDM v8 DF-PRT-008 to DF-PRT-016 and the relationship row 'partner_instruction / case_id / recovery_case / N:1 / Yes'; Physical Schema v6.sql CREATE TABLE partner_instruction; Architecture v5 Text Extract v1 slide 29; Template and Rules DP-01 and DP-02
- Proposed fix: Keep case_id as a varchar(255) business key with no foreign key, as every other service does, and enforce the link at the contract.
- Owner role: Data architect with the platform DB owner
