## 1. GAP-1104 (Register gap; ruling R-28632; sections: find it)
- Title: No source gives the alert thresholds, the reviewed time of the late WIN batch or the Schedule state per environment
- Detail: The deployment alerts on a file that is not delivered, a pod batch that is not COMPLETE, a dispatch count of zero inside the window, the outbox lag and a stuck workflow, and it must also hold a reviewed time after which the batch of the day counts as late. No source gives a threshold, a window, a band, an evaluation interval or that time: the legacy story names a stale period of one business day and an attempt limit of three, and the FSD names the stale message, but neither gives an operational value.
- Evidence: Legacy RWB-E1-055 section 8 BR7: "The notice data product is stale one business day after the WIN batch was due"; FSD P6 v3 A2.2 VR-P6-002-1: "Data product stale after a late or failed ACE batch for its source (`v_ace_batch_status`)"; Architecture v5 slide 44: "JOB-21 Stuck workflow scan every 5 min ...
- Proposed fix: Set the alert values and the reviewed time of the late batch in the rwb-comms values files, confirm them at UAT with the service owner and the platform observability owner, and record each agreed value in the runbook entry its alert links.
- Owner role: DevOps lead

## 2. GAP-1105 (Register gap; ruling R-28633; sections: find it)
- Title: Only one hold reason is named for a notice
- Detail: The notice carries hold_reason, and the state model of the loop includes a Held state, but the sources name exactly one value, TEMPLATE_WITHDRAWN, on a template that is withdrawn after approval. The other reasons a notice may be held - a conduct block, a consent stop, a failed decision record, an address that cannot be composed - appear as refusal paths in the FSD and the legacy story without a value on hold_reason, so the list, its owner and its version cannot be fixed from a source and the state table of this story carries one named value only.
- Evidence: FSD P6 v3 A2.2 AC-EXC-P6-002-02: "notice \"N-0101\" is \"Held\" with hold reason \"TEMPLATE_WITHDRAWN\""; TSD P6 v2 T3.1: "DF-LEG-032 / hold_reason / TEXT / varchar(30) / No / None / Workbench" — no LOV is named for the field.
- Proposed fix: Publish the hold reason list as a versioned reference list with TEMPLATE_WITHDRAWN as its first row, and add its other values with the business owner before the Epic 2 dispatch refinements land; until then a hold writes the one named value or a free-text reason from the holding rule.
- Owner role: Business analyst, P6

## 3. GAP-2246 (Register gap; ruling R-28634; sections: find it)
- Title: The timer has no field for the notice it belongs to, and its field ID is unallocated
- Detail: The legacy story writes the notice reference on the notice row of the P6 module, and the ownership map gives the timer table to rwb-case with id, case_id, timer_type, started_at, expires_at, warn_at, paused, status, escalation_level, warned_at, treatment_id, escalated_to_role and escalated_at. A notice period that cannot name its notice is of no use in a court pack, so the story adds one nullable column and one index; the Data Dictionary field ID for that column is not allocated in any source read here.
- Evidence: TSD P3 v3 T3.1 lists DF-WRK-027 to DF-WRK-036, DF-WRK-041 to DF-WRK-044 for timer with no reference column; RWB-E1-055 section 11 reads DF-LEG-015 notice.id and DF-LEG-016 notice.case_id only.
- Proposed fix: Allocate the field ID in the Logical Data Model and add it to the ownership map at the next revision; the column, its type and its index stand as written.
- Owner role: rwb-case data owner and the Data Dictionary owner
