## 2. GAP-1782 (Register gap; ruling R-29232; sections: find it)
- Title: AccountsTransferred has no event number and neither debt sale event has a published schema
- Detail: The event catalogue gives EV-23 LotAwarded a row with producer rwb-debtsale and consumers rwb-case and rwb-payment, and lists AccountsTransferred under the unnumbered 'plus' rows with rwb-case and rwb-reporting as consumers. EV-25 ProceedsMatched is the only event this service consumes. No AsyncAPI document, no CloudEvents schema and no payload field list exists for either debt sale event in the sources read, although the FSD requires the case steps to follow the award and the transfer.
- Evidence: Arch v5 slide 43 (row 23 LotAwarded; row 25 ProceedsMatched; '+' row AccountsTransferred with consumers rwb-case and rwb-reporting); Ownership Map Events: EV-23 only. Program BRD v5 section 10.4 (events are CloudEvents on rwb.<short>.events.v1 with an outbox and an inbox).
- Proposed fix: Publish both events as CloudEvents 1.0 on rwb.debtsale.events.v1 keyed by caseId, with BACKWARD-compatible schemas in the registry and no personal data in either; give AccountsTransferred its catalogue number under the same convention.
- Owner role: Solution architect
