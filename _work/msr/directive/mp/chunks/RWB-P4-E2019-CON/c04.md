## 1. GAP-1567 (Register gap; ruling R-28516; sections: find it)
- Title: lov_value and partner_user are defined in the P4 technical specification but owned by rwb-case and rwb-partner
- Detail: The P4 technical specification defines a list-of-values table and a partner user table with their columns and a relationship from partner_user to partner. The Ownership Map rules the list of values to rwb-case as reference data and the partner user to rwb-partner, and the PRD therefore treats both as referenced entities owned elsewhere. The consequence is that rwb-access reads the fourteen capability areas, the four scopes, the role classes and the partner identity attributes through another service's contract, and needs an agreed read path and a decision on what it caches; the matrix seed and ...
- Evidence: TSD P4 T3.1 'lov_value (NEW). A value in a list of values' with DF-REF-027 to 033 and 'partner_user (NEW). A partner user's identity bound to one partner firm and one partner role' with DF-REF-212 to 217; T3.3 relationship partner_user to partner; Ownership Map: 'lov_value is reference data and stay ...
- Proposed fix: Drop lov_value and partner_user from TSD P4 T3.1 and keep the lists of values in TSD P3 and the partner identity in TSD S9; state in the rwb-access contract which list-of-values versions and which partner identity attributes it reads and how often it refreshes them, so the matrix seed and the session answer are built against one source.
- Owner role: Solution architect

## 2. GAP-0786 (Register gap; ruling R-28791; sections: find it)
- Title: The certificate profile, the rotation and revocation of the rwb-access hop, the numeric rate limit and the namespace and service names of the DMZ hop are specified by no source
- Detail: Four infrastructure values of the hop from the partner gateway to rwb-access are absent from every source. (a) The certificate profile of the hop, its lifetime, its rotation window and the revocation propagation are named for the partner client at the gateway by RWB-PLT-N007-INF and are not stated for the gateway-to-rwb-access hop, so this story applies one profile to both ends and fixes the rotation inside an overlap window with a suspension at the deadline. (b) The per-client rate limit, its burst, its window and its retry hint on the resolution route are stated by no source: RWB-PLT-N007-IN ...
- Evidence: RWB-PLT-N007-INF section 11 reads the certificate as valid for 12 months and the revocation as effective within 30 days at the latest, for the partner client; its section 12.2 names rwb-partner as the only upstream and no rwb-access hop; Architecture v5 slide 29 reads one login per service in one AC ...
- Proposed fix: At the security design authority review of RWB-PLT-N007-INF, extend the certificate profile and the rotation window to the rwb-access hop, record the per-client limit and its burst for the resolution route in the gateway configuration baseline, and publish the namespace, service and port of rwb-access with the platform deployment so the NetworkPolicy names them from a source. Nothing here blocks the build: every value is configuration and the recorded defaults are the values the tests use.
- Owner role: SCB IT infrastructure with the security design authority
