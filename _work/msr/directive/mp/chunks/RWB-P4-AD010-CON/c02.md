## 8. GAP-0818 (Register gap; ruling R-23024; sections: find it)
- Title: An approval given before the valid-from date is not specified
- Detail: The legacy criterion says an approved version takes effect from its valid-from date. No source says what happens when the approval is given before that date: whether the approval is refused until the date, whether the version sits in APPROVED until the date, or whether it goes straight to IN_FORCE. TSD T4 lists APPROVED and IN_FORCE as separate states but gives no rule for the gap, and the Drools design only states the approve-and-date discipline.
- Evidence: Legacy RWB-AD-010 AC1; TSD P4 v3 T4 (LOADED, APPROVED by a second person, IN_FORCE from its valid-from date); Temporal and Drools Design Text Extract v1 slide 6 note (delegation limits follow the same approve-and-date discipline as the Drools packages); R-UNI-38
- Proposed fix: A version takes effect from its valid_from date only and never retroactively; an approval given before that date is allowed, the version reads APPROVED until the date and IN_FORCE from it, and the version it supersedes keeps valid_to set to the day before
- Owner role: Business analyst, P4
