## 6. GAP-2046 (Register gap; ruling R-23195; sections: find it)
- Title: The Jobs row still reads the Quartz job table, which the target architecture retires
- Detail: Version 3 measures job runs, duration and missed runs from the Quartz job table. Architecture v5 retires the shared Quartz and batch tables: each job is a Temporal Schedule or an event-started workflow in the owning service, JOB-05 is retired because the timers are durable timers inside the workflow, and the job map gives one owner per job (slide 44; DP-10; ADR-016). The Quartz table therefore has no rows to measure.
- Evidence: v3 section 1 row 'Jobs' ('Quartz job table'); Architecture v5 slide 44 (job map with the mechanism per job, 'JOB-05 Timer check ... durable timers (retired)', 'The ACE shared Quartz and batch tables are no longer used by Recovery'); Rules v1 R5.5 DP-10.
- Proposed fix: Version 4 reads the Jobs row as the Temporal Schedules and event-started workflows of the owning service, with the job map of Architecture v5 slide 44 as the owner list and the job catalogue of P2 rwb-integration as the register.
- Owner role: Platform team with the DevOps team
