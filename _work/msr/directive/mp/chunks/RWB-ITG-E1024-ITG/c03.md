## 1. GAP-1594 (Register gap; ruling R-22031; sections: find it)
- Title: The FSD identifies the customer on a document by the ACE person key and the TSD by party id
- Detail: The FSD v3 change note states that document.party_id becomes the business key document.person_key under D-RCV-35 and ADR-010, and its field catalog gives person_key as a varchar(255) with no foreign key. The TSD P5 v2 still carries DF-DOC-003 party_id of type uuid with a relationship to the ACE party table. Under R-PREC the TSD ranks above the FSD, yet the TSD value is the position the FSD explicitly replaced, and a relationship to an ACE table is forbidden by DP-01.
- Evidence: FSD P5 v3 change note: document.party_id becomes the business key document.person_key. A2.3.3.2: document.person_key varchar(255), the ACE customer this record belongs to (v_ace_customer.person_key), business key, no foreign key. TSD P5 v2 DF-DOC-003: party_id CROSS_REF uuid; T3.3: document party_id ...
- Proposed fix: The PRD takes person_key as the business key of varchar(255) with no foreign key, because the FSD change is the later intent and a foreign key into ACE is forbidden. OQ-P5-14 carries the confirmation and the TSD needs the same correction.
- Owner role: Data architect
