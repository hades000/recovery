## 1. GAP-1076 (Register gap; ruling R-28808; sections: find it)
- Title: The message delivery status vocabulary conflicts with LOV-DELIVERY
- Detail: The legacy sets the delivery status of a message to Sent, then Delivered, Read or Failed, and maps the NSS result code to SENT, DELIVERED and FAILED and the LINE OA status to DELIVERED and READ. TSD P6 T3.2 LOV-DELIVERY holds DELIVERED, REFUSED, UNDELIVERABLE and PENDING for the notice and WIN proof of delivery, so it carries neither SENT nor FAILED nor READ and it is the only list the module defines.
- Evidence: legacy section 9: 'Sets the delivery status on the ledger entry: Sent, then Delivered, Read or Failed'; TSD P6 T3.2 'LOV-DELIVERY / DELIVERED, REFUSED, UNDELIVERABLE, PENDING'; legacy section 13 result-code maps
- Proposed fix: The message status list is held as its own list in RWB-P6-E2022-CON (SENT, DELIVERED, FAILED, READ) and LOV-DELIVERY is left to the notice and proof-of-delivery capability; the data steward confirms or names the list.
- Owner role: Data steward

- Detail: Two acceptance criteria state that a send is retried until the retry limit of 5 is reached and then placed in the exception queue (NSS and the e-signature path). Program BRD v5 NFR-026 ('Three retries per item, then the exception queue'). Arch v5 slide 13 (retry 1 min, retry 10 min, retry  ...
- Proposed fix: Use the NFR-026 limit of three retries on the platform schedule and keep the fifth-attempt wording as the source text of the acceptance criteria, with the number confirmed by the solution architect before wave 2.
- Owner role: Solution architect

## 3. GAP-1601 (Register gap; ruling R-28810; sections: find it)
- Title: The functional specification names JOB-11 as the retry job for sends, and Architecture v5 makes JOB-11 the outbox relay in every service
- Detail: The functional specification retries a failed SMS or e-signature send through JOB-11 on its schedule. Architecture v5 slide 44 makes JOB-11 the outbox sender in every service (outbox relay to Kafka, every minute), and the Ownership Map gives it to the platform. The retry of an outbound notification is therefore not JOB-11 in v5, and the mechanism that retries it is not named.
- Evidence: FSD P6 AC-EXC-P6-003-03 ('JOB-11 retries the send on its schedule') and AC-EXC-P6-008-03. Arch v5 slide 44 (JOB-11 Outbox sender, every minute, every service, outbox relay to Kafka) and slide 20 (every service: outbox relay). Ownership Map: JOB-11 owner PLT, no Temporal Schedule (ASM-A6-flows-9, ASM ...
- Proposed fix: Read the JOB-11 reference as the platform retry and outbox mechanism, keep JOB-10 as this service's only schedule (WIN notices, 16:00), and name no second job for notification retries.
- Owner role: Solution architect
