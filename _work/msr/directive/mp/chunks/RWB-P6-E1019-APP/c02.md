## 4. GAP-0679 (Register gap; ruling R-21988; sections: 11)
- Title: The column-level split of the legacy role and permission tables between rwb-case and rwb-access is not defined
- Detail: The ownership map puts role, permission, masking_rule and delegation_limit in rwb-access (P4), and the legacy story reads and writes ROLE and TEAM rows inside one schema. No source states which columns of the legacy role and TEAM tables move to rwb-access, which stay in rwb-case reference data, or how a queue row's role code is validated after the move.
- Evidence: Ownership map: role and permission owner P4 with basis Arch v5 slides 14 and 41; TSD P3 v3 T3.2 keeps TEAM and ROLE as LOV-REF-TABLE codes while TSD P4 v3 declares the role table; legacy RWB-E1-019 BR1, BR9 and §11.
- Proposed fix: Roles, permissions, scope, masking and delegation stay with rwb-access; rwb-case keeps TEAM as reference data and keeps only role_code as a code, validated at load time over the rwb-access authorise contract (DP-08). Confirm the column list with P4 in the RWB-P4-E1037-CON contract.
- Owner role: Solution architect
