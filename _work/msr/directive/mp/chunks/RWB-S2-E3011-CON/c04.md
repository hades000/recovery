## 1. GAP-1700 (Register gap; ruling R-28885; sections: find it)
- Title: TreatmentClosed has no event number and no row in the Ownership Map event sheet
- Detail: Arch v5 slide 18 gives rwb-treatment the OUT topic rwb.treatment.events.v1 with the events TreatmentApproved and TreatmentClosed, and slide 43 lists TreatmentApproved as row 10 (producer rwb-treatment, consumers rwb-comms and rwb-payment) and carries 'TreatmentClosed, GuaranteeClaim*' in the additional rows, produced by rwb-treatment or rwb-guarantee with rwb-case and rwb-reporting as consumers. The Ownership Map holds EV-10 TreatmentApproved only. The event this service publishes when a treatment closes therefore has no number, no producer and no consumer that can be tested.
- Evidence: Arch v5 slide 18 (EVT rwb.treatment.events.v1: TreatmentApproved, TreatmentClosed), slide 43 row 10 (TreatmentApproved) and the additional row (TreatmentClosed, GuaranteeClaim*). Ownership Map Events: EV-10 owner S2; no TreatmentClosed row.
- Proposed fix: Publish TreatmentClosed on rwb.treatment.events.v1 keyed by caseId, CloudEvents, through the outbox, and carry it without an EV number until the catalogue allocates one; rwb-case and rwb-reporting consume it.
- Owner role: Solution architect
