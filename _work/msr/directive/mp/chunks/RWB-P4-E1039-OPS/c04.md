## 8. GAP-2342 (Register gap; ruling R-28594; sections: find it)
- Title: The incident queue name and the session-answer reuse bound of the content read are not specified
- Detail: The legacy story raises an incident to the platform administrator queue when the access entry cannot be written, and this story repeats that path when the record fails. - Evidence: Legacy RWB-E1-039 X1: 'the read or export returns no data; status 503; an incident is raised'; AC7: 'the system raises an incident to the platform administrator queue'; no queue name, reuse bound or parameter key appears in TSD P5 v2 or in RWB-P4-E1039-CON.
- Proposed fix: Name the incident queue and the reuse bound in the platform observability and parameters baseline, and record them as parameter rows; until then the queue is a parameter of this service and the session answer is reused within one request only.
- Owner role: DevOps team
