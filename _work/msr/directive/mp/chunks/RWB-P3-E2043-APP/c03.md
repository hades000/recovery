## 2. GAP-2016 (Register gap; ruling R-22047; sections: find it)
- Title: Timer row and durable timer kept in step by one activity
- Detail: Version 3 keeps every timer as a row in the timer table (E1-028) written through the TimerScheduler port, with JOB-05 as the five-minute check. No source states which of the two is authoritative when they disagree, or how a pause, a resume and a cancel are applied to both without a second write path.
- Evidence: Workflow Engine Design v3 section 4: 'Timers stay rows in the timer table (E1-028)' and 'JOB-05 checks due timers every five minutes and calls the port'. Architecture v5 slide 44, JOB-05: 'durable timers (retired)'. Architecture v5 slide 25: 'Timers that last for years'.
- Proposed fix: The durable timer is authoritative and the timer row is the audit copy, written by ApplyStateActivity in the same transaction as the state, and read back on a reopen.
- Owner role: Service owner P3 rwb-case with the platform team

## 9. GAP-2019 (Register gap; ruling R-22737; sections: find it)
- Title: Child workflow start policy and failure path are not specified

## 10. GAP-2018 (Register gap; ruling R-22738; sections: find it)
- Title: Strict-mode wait at a point of no return is not defined in this design
- Detail: Version 3 makes the decision log write the gate of the chain and stops the chain when it fails. Architecture v5 records the decision locally in the same transaction as the state change and seals it centrally in rwb-decision, with strict mode at the points of no return. No source states what the workflow does while it waits for the ledger to confirm: whether the activity retries, how long it waits, what the timeout is, and which steps are pivots in workflow terms.
- Evidence: Workflow Engine Design v3 section 3, WriteDecisionLogActivity: 'This is the gate for the rest of the chain'. Architecture v5 slide 28: 'Strict mode for points of no return ... wait until the central ledger confirms the record'. Temporal and Drools Design v1 slide 26 lists the pivots per saga.
- Proposed fix: A pivot activity waits on the ledger acknowledgement with the retry policy of section 7 and a documented timeout, and the workflow fails with an incident when the timeout is reached, without repeating a completed step.
- Owner role: Service owner P7 rwb-decision with the saga owners S2, S5, S6 and S7
