## 3. GAP-1567 (Register gap; ruling R-28516; sections: find it)
- Title: lov_value and partner_user are defined in the P4 technical specification but owned by rwb-case and rwb-partner
- Detail: The P4 technical specification defines a list-of-values table and a partner user table with their columns and a relationship from partner_user to partner. The Ownership Map rules the list of values to rwb-case as reference data and the partner user to rwb-partner, and the PRD therefore treats both as referenced entities owned elsewhere. The consequence is that rwb-access reads the fourteen capability areas, the four scopes, the role classes and the partner identity attributes through another service's contract, and needs an agreed read path and a decision on what it caches; the matrix seed and ...
- Evidence: TSD P4 T3.1 'lov_value (NEW). A value in a list of values' with DF-REF-027 to 033 and 'partner_user (NEW). A partner user's identity bound to one partner firm and one partner role' with DF-REF-212 to 217; T3.3 relationship partner_user to partner; Ownership Map: 'lov_value is reference data and stay ...
- Proposed fix: Drop lov_value and partner_user from TSD P4 T3.1 and keep the lists of values in TSD P3 and the partner identity in TSD S9; state in the rwb-access contract which list-of-values versions and which partner identity attributes it reads and how often it refreshes them, so the matrix seed and the session answer are built against one source.
- Owner role: Solution architect
