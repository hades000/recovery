## 2. GAP-1623 (Register gap; ruling R-22046; sections: find it)
- Title: The Waiting approval case state and the approval task are set inside P7 while the case state and the queue belong to rwb-case
- Detail: The approval criteria require the case status to become Waiting approval when an approval is routed, and the FSD describes the service raising a task where a person is needed. The case state machine, the queues and the tasks are rwb-case's under Architecture v5 and the ownership map, and this service writes only rwb_decision. Either this service calls the case contract to move the state and raise the task, or P3 moves the state on ApprovalRequested; the PRD cannot state one and the backlog cannot estimate the approval slice until it is fixed.
- Evidence: FSD P7 AC-P7-015-01 ends 'And the case status becomes "Waiting approval"'; Arch v5 slide 41 gives rwb-case 'case, CMMN state, queues, tasks, allocation, timers'; the Ownership Map lists case and task under P3; TSD P7 T4 reads 'No case state machine of its own'.
- Proposed fix: Decide in the case contract whether the state move and the task are a P3 reaction to ApprovalRequested (preferred, one writer of the case state) or a P3 operation this service calls in the same step; record the choice in the contract and in the FSD acceptance criteria of A2.15 and A2.16.
- Owner role: Solution architect with the rwb-case contract owner
