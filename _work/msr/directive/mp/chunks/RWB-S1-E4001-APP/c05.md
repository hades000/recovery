## 3. GAP-2593 (Register gap; ruling R-28888; sections: find it)
- Title: No source names the event or the timer that starts the age check on a completed ACE batch, and S1 owns no job and no event
- Detail: FSD S1 A2.1.2 names three possible triggers of the step, 'a completed ACE batch, an event or a timer', and A2.1.4.4 declares no background event for the feature. - Owner role: S1 rwb-valuation service owner with the P1 rwb-source-data owner
