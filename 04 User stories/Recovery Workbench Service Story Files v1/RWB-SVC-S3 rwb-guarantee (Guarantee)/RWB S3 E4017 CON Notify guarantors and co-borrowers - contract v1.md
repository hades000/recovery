### 1. Story

As a **contract owner of rwb-guarantee**, I want **the published shape of the demand to a guarantor or a co-borrower — the `GuarantorNotice` element, the approved-template rule, the activity input that reaches rwb-comms, the notice-period key, the record rule and the fakes — instead of the monolith's in-process send with no stated template and no stated period**, so that **the notice story, the negotiation story, the letter's owner and the wave 3 integration harness can build against one frozen payload and one stated template rule from Day 1, and no template code and no notice period is ever hard-coded**.

The contract covers exactly five things:

- the `GuarantorNotice` element and its seven properties, and the operation it belongs to — the notice form of `PATCH /guarantee-claims/{claimId}`, declared by RWB-S3-E4013-CON and named, not redeclared, here (DP-13);
- the approved-template rule: the demand is issued only from a template that is approved and current for the product and the notice type, the answer is accepted with a dispatch reference or refused with a reason code, and no wording, name or address crosses the boundary (FSD S3 A2.5.2; BR-S3-005);
- the delivery path: the activity input that carries the payload to the queue `rwb-comms` through the workflow kit's Notify template, since Architecture v5 slide 13 gives S3 a Temporal line to P6 and no REST line, and the Ownership Map gives S3 no event to publish (ASM-4524);
- the notice-period key: the value in force the routing workbook calls "notice period by product", exported as a key and never as a number, and the notice due date computed from it that the payload carries;
- the versioning rule and the fakes: major version 1, the pinning rule and the drift test, and the five fakes every consumer builds against.

It does not own: the operation, its `GuaranteeClaim` response, its envelope and its error set, which are RWB-S3-E4013-CON; the notice branch of the workflow, the dispatch loop, the record write and the timer itself, which are RWB-S3-E4017-APP; the claim entity and its states, which are RWB-S3-E4013-APP and RWB-S3-E4013-CON; the template wording, the channel, the addressee resolution and the proof of delivery, which are rwb-comms' (P6); the case, the party and the case clock, which are rwb-case's (DP-01; ASM-4525); the decision-record library, which is RWB-PLT-E1027-CON; and SCR-17 and its route, which are the platform edge story's and the screen owners' (DP-19).

### 2. Business rationale

The requirement this feature serves is one sentence, and it fixes both the generation and the path: guarantor and co-borrower notices are generated by rule and sent on the same path as customer notices (REQ-S3-004, FSD S3 "Requirements served"). The feature description adds the demand: sends the demand to every guarantor and co-borrower on the case, from the approved template (FSD S3 A2.5.2). The measure the module carries is claims filed inside the deadline at 100 percent, and the problem the module exists to fix is that guarantee and TCG claims are late because deadlines are not tracked (FSD S3 A2.1.1, A1.3). None of that has a shape today: no source publishes a notice body, a template status rule, a notice-period key or a delivery contract, and the legacy story carries only the sentence, the ACs and the endpoint table (GAP-2726).

In the monolith the send was an in-process call in one deployable, so no payload was ever written down and the template was whatever the code held. Architecture v5 changes all three pieces: S3 owns two claim operations on its own base and no notice operation (Architecture v5 slide 13; Ownership Map rows of S3), the service publishes no event, so the demand cannot leave as an event (Ownership Map), and the rwb-comms call is a Temporal one, since the slide draws T and E for S3 to P6 and no R (Architecture v5 slide 13). A published payload on the owner's queue is therefore the only place where the officer's action, the letter and the record meet.

Two facts make the contract urgent rather than tidy. The first is the template. The legacy story says "the approved template" and the FSD repeats it, but no source lists the notice types, the template codes or the rule that decides what is current; left to the implementers, one team passes a template name, another a letter code and the record stops proving which wording went out, which contradicts BR-S3-005 — no value a business person may change is written in code — and AC1 (GAP-2726; ASM-4524). The second is the period. The routing workbook carries the notice period only as "Notice period by product" in its service-level column, with no parameter key and no column on `guarantee_claim`, so the contract exports the key and states that the notice due date is computed from it and recorded with the notice (GAP-2726; ASM-4526). The addressee is the third: the legacy data table names `v_ace_customer` because the monolith filled the letter from it, and in the target the views are read only by rwb-source-data, so the payload carries a party reference and the name and the address are resolved where the letter is built (Architecture v5 slide 8; DP-05; ASM-4524).

The fake is the Day-1 unblocker. RWB-S3-E4017-APP, the negotiation story, rwb-comms' letter work and the wave 3 integration harness can build and test the demand, the template answer and the refusal paths before any live route exists, and the notice story can prove its own contract conformance before an environment is booked (Template and Rules §R5.4 class C, §R5.7).

### 3. Preconditions

- The claim operation exists and is another contract's: `PATCH /guarantee-claims/{claimId}` on base `/api/recovery/guarantee/v1`, declared by RWB-S3-E4013-CON with its envelope, its `GuaranteeClaim` response and its error set (DP-13; TSD S3 T-A.1 API-S3-002).
- The claim field set exists and has no notice field: `guarantee_claim` with the eight fields of TSD S3 T3.1 (DF-TRT-015 to DF-TRT-022) in schema `rwb_guarantee`, owned by S3 (DP-01).
- The service owns no event: the Ownership Map gives S3 zero published events, so this contract declares no topic body and no key, and the demand leaves as a Temporal activity (ASM-4524).
- The notice period is named by no model: the routing workbook carries "Notice period by product" in its service-level column only, so the key is this contract's working read (GAP-2726; ASM-4526).
- The template registry is rwb-comms': the current template for a product and a notice type, its wording, its channel and its proof of delivery are the owner's, and this contract fixes only the code that addresses it (ASM-4524).
- The party and its role arrive on the case signal from rwb-case, so the payload carries a party reference and never a name, an address or a customer identifier (DP-01, DP-05; ASM-4525).
- The decision record is written through RWB-PLT-E1027-CON with `@Transactional(propagation = MANDATORY)`, refusing with MSG-P7-009 and failing with MSG-P7-003; `decision_record` is a library object in `rwb_guarantee` and `decision_log_entry` is P7's (DP-03, DP-01).
- The gateway-bff composite route of RWB-PLT-N001-CON exists, so SCR-17's binding to the notice facts is named and not declared here (DP-19).
- The access rule is the platform guard library's: capability area "Treatment and offer", right U for the notice form and right C for the claim create, with RW-GUARANT and RW-CASEOWN holding CRU (TSD S3 T6.1; ASM-4525).
- The legacy message texts this payload raises are kept unchanged: MSG-GEN-001, MSG-P4-002, MSG-P7-003 and MSG-P1-001.
- No build dependency and no approval stop: a CON story is Day 1 by rule (Template and Rules §R5.7) and is published at status Draft (realignment v1).

### 4. Flow

1. The contract author fixes the element: `GuarantorNotice` with seven properties, their types taken from TSD S3 T3.1 where a field exists and from the case signal where it does not, and nothing else.
2. The author fixes the template rule: the template must be approved and current for the product and the notice type, the answer is accepted with a dispatch reference or refused with a reason code, and the wording, the name and the address never cross the boundary.
3. The author writes the delivery section: the queue `rwb-comms`, the kit's Notify template, the activity input, the one-call-per-addressee rule and the statement that this service publishes no event.
4. The author writes the period section: the key, the computation of the notice due date, the durable timer input and the statement that no `timer` row and no Schedule exists.
5. The author writes the operation section: the path, the method, the right and the idempotency header, all named as RWB-S3-E4013-CON's, with this contract's payload attached to it.
6. The author writes the record section: the facts the record must carry, the `MANDATORY` rule and the two failure messages.
7. The author writes the versioning and pinning section: major version 1, the compatible-versus-breaking rule and the pinning obligation of the consumers.
8. The schema and lint steps run: the payload validated against the element and the claim field set, the payload scanned for a personal value, and a payload carrying a name or an address refused.
9. The fakes are generated from the same example set: the notice fake, the decision-record fake, the rwb-comms activity stub, the case-signal mock and the route fake.
10. The drift test runs the fakes against the document on every change and fails the build on any difference.
11. The document and the fakes are published at v1, and RWB-S3-E4017-APP, the negotiation story, the letter owner and the wave 3 integration harness pin the version.

### 12. Contract

#### 12.1 REST operations provided

Owner rwb-guarantee (S3); base `/api/recovery/guarantee/v1` (DP-13). This contract provides the notice payload of one operation whose path, envelope, response and error set are declared by RWB-S3-E4013-CON; it declares no operation of its own and no second path.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Operation | The notice form of `PATCH /guarantee-claims/{claimId}`, declared by RWB-S3-E4013-CON and named here |
| Body | `GuarantorNotice` |
| Response | `GuaranteeClaim` of RWB-S3-E4013-CON, with the claim status and the deadline of the row |
| Access | Capability area Treatment and offer, right U, scope applied, checked by the platform guard library |
| Idempotency | `Idempotency-Key` header of the operation |
| Errors | The error set of RWB-S3-E4013-CON: 400 or 422 with MSG-GEN-001 and the field, 401, 403 with MSG-P4-002, 404, 409, 503, and MSG-P7-003 and MSG-P7-009 on the record path |

| Property | Type | Required | Maps to | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `claimId` | uuid | Yes | DF-TRT-015 `id` | The claim the demand belongs to |
| `caseId` | uuid | Yes | DF-TRT-016 `case_id` | The case the demand is issued on |
| `partyRef` | uuid | Yes | `case_party.id` of rwb-case, carried as a reference | One addressee; a reference and never a name |
| `partyRole` | varchar(20) LOV-PARTY-ROLE | Yes | `case_party.party_role` of rwb-case, carried as a code | Guarantor, co-borrower or corporate guarantor, the three roles R-GUA-06 names |
| `noticeType` | varchar(20) LOV-NOTICE-TYPE | Yes | none | The D6.6 notice type of R-GUA-06; the list is the owner's reference data |
| `templateCode` | varchar(30) | Yes | none | The approved template the owner holds as current for the product and the notice type |
| `noticeDueDate` | date | Yes | none | The notice-period start plus the period in force; no `guarantee_claim` column holds it |

The element is this contract's working read. No source document fixes a notice payload: TSD S3 T3.1 gives `guarantee_claim` eight fields and none is a notice field, FSD S3 A2.5 names the demand without a shape, and the legacy story names only the sentence, the ACs and the endpoint table. The seven properties are therefore published with the gap and the assumption, and every one of them either maps to a field or is stated as a reference or a computed value (GAP-2726; ASM-4527). The path is the one RWB-S3-E4013-CON publishes; the claim create form named by TSD S3 T-A.1 API-S3-001 is that contract's and is not republished here.

#### 12.2 REST operations consumed

None. The contract calls no service. The party reference and its role arrive on the case signal from rwb-case, the period in force arrives from the parameters contract as a value the notice story passes in, and the record is written in process through RWB-PLT-E1027-CON (DP-03; ASM-4525, ASM-4526). The delivery to rwb-comms is a Temporal activity and not a consumed REST operation, which is why the contract dependencies of this story are None (BR4).

#### 12.3 Events published

None. The Ownership Map gives S3 no published event, `rwb.guarantee.events.v1` carries none of this feature, and this contract therefore declares no event name, no topic body and no key; the demand leaves as the workflow kit's Notify activity on the queue `rwb-comms` (BR4; Architecture v5 slide 13; ASM-4524). The local decision record rides the library's shared topic `rwb.decision-record.v1`, which belongs to RWB-PLT-E1027-CON and is not an event of this service (DP-03).

#### 12.4 Events consumed

None. The guarantee claim's triggers reach the stage as signals forwarded by the parent `CaseWorkflow`, and the notice clock is the workflow's own durable timer. The rwb-comms and rwb-payment events that Architecture v5 slide 13 shows S3 listening to belong to the claim tracking and the post-payment paths of the same feature family (BR4; ASM-4524).

#### 12.5 Workflows and activities

None of its own. The contract defines no workflow and no activity; it fixes the input of one activity that RWB-S3-E4017-APP calls.

### 13. Solution plan

**Package.** Contract artefact of rwb-guarantee: the `GuarantorNotice` type in `th.co.scb.ace.workbench.recovery.guarantee.api`, the OpenAPI fragment of the notice form merged into the service document of RWB-S3-E4013-CON, the activity input schema of `notifyGuarantor`, and the template, delivery, period, versioning and fake sections in the contract document. Service base `/api/recovery/guarantee/v1`, schema `rwb_guarantee`, topic `rwb.guarantee.events.v1`, task queue `rwb-guarantee` in namespace `rwb-recovery` (DP-13, DP-14). The implementation is RWB-S3-E4017-APP.

**Pattern application**

| DP | Legacy marker | Microservice pattern | Dependency added |
|---|---|---|---|
| DP-03 | DecisionLogWriter, decision log | Local decision-record library and topic `rwb.decision-record.v1` | H RWB-PLT-E1027-CON |
| DP-05 | AceSourceQuery, `v_ace_*`, account_ext | Read through rwb-source-data's contract or the service's own ACE views; this payload carries no ACE fact, because the addressee belongs to the letter | none |
| DP-13 | `/api/recovery/v1` | `/api/recovery/<short>/v1` | none |
| DP-19 | Screen, ace-web | gateway-bff composite route | H RWB-PLT-N001-CON |

The four rows are referred to here, not carried: this contract builds against nothing, and the two H dependencies are the consuming story's, which is why its own build list is empty by rule (Template and Rules §R5.7). DP-03 fixes the record rule the notice must satisfy, DP-05 is applied by removal because no `v_ace_*` fact appears in the payload, DP-13 fixes the base the payload is published under, and DP-19 is the route through which SCR-17 binds the notice facts. The legacy in-process send is carried by DP-11's one-hop rule in its Temporal form, because Architecture v5 slide 13 makes the rwb-comms call a Temporal one and not REST (ASM-4524).

**Database.** No change. `guarantee_claim` keeps the eight fields of TSD S3 T3.1 and the standard columns; `claimId`, `caseId` and the deadline are the only payload properties with a column, and `partyRef`, `partyRole`, `noticeType`, `templateCode` and `noticeDueDate` have none, so the notice facts are recorded on the decision record (AC4; BR6, BR7; GAP-2726; ASM-4527). The contract requires that no migration adds a notice column and that `decision_record` stays the library's object.

**Configuration and reference data.** None of its own. The notice period by product, the notice types, the party roles and the template codes are values in force of the parameters, the rule package and the owner's reference data, and this contract exports their keys rather than their values (BR5; ASM-4526). The capability area and rights of the operation are the platform guard library's rows (BR9).

### 15. Screens and states

No screen in this story and none declared. SCR-17 Guarantee claim (Not due; due; submitted; paid; refused) shows the claim, its deadline and the notice facts this payload allows — the template code, the addressee count and any refused addressee — through the gateway-bff composite route, and it is built by the screen owners and the platform edge story (DP-19; RWB-PLT-N001-CON). The stage state `GUARANTOR_NOTICE` is not one of the five screen states and this contract does not add a sixth; the mapping of the stage vocabulary onto the screen's states is not specified in the source documents (GAP-2726; ASM-4525). ### 17. Guardrails

- No business value in the payload: the period, the dates, the template codes and the reason-code list are values in force, and the contract fixes only types, keys and rules (BR5).
- No personal value in the payload, the activity input, an example, a fake or a log: the addressee belongs to the letter and the scan fails the build otherwise (BR8).
- No notice column and no due-date column: the notice facts live on the decision record and the period is a parameter (BR6, BR7).
- No path, no response and no error code of this contract's own: the operation, its envelope and its statuses belong to RWB-S3-E4013-CON (BR1).
- No event invented and none declared: this service owns no published event and the delivery is a Temporal activity (BR4).
- No cross-schema reference and no foreign key across services; the boundary test fails the build (DP-01).
- No case table read and no party name in the payload: the reference and the role arrive on the signal (BR12; ASM-4525).
- No record written outside the library's `MANDATORY` transaction, and no ledger row: `decision_log_entry` is P7's and is named here only (DP-03; DP-01).
- A published version is never edited in place: a breaking change raises major version 2 with a migration note (BR10).
