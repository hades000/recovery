**Change log.** D-RCV-60, 22 November 2026: split from RWB-E2-014 for rwb-decision, reworked onto the approval table and the decision-record library, with the notice served by rwb-comms and the screens served through the gateway-bff composite route. Tool: T3 Code / OpenCode / DeepSeek v4.1 Flash.

### 1. Story

As a **litigation officer (RW-LITOFF)**, I want **one screen in rwb-decision that shows the demand or recall notice generated by rwb-comms, the case facts it was built from with the business date of each, and the rule and the template version that proposed it, with approve, change or reject and a reason**, so that **the first decision in the Workbench runs with a person approving inside the service that owns approvals, and only an approved notice reaches the 16:00 dispatch run**.

### 2. Business rationale

A demand fee notice sent to a customer incomplete is a Bank of Thailand finding, and a wrong amount or address on a notice can void the notice period and delay litigation. The legacy story checked notices on paper, in one monolith, where the notice, the case, the ACE balance and the approval could be read in one transaction and where nothing had to be agreed between owners. In the target that transaction does not exist: rwb-decision owns the approval and the central decision ledger in `rwb_decision`, rwb-comms owns the notice and its template version in `rwb_comms`, rwb-case owns the case and its routing, rwb-source-data owns the ACE account and contact facts, and gateway-bff composes the screen from the owners one hop each (Architecture v5 slides 14, 17, 20 and 41; TSD P7 v2 T1 and T5.2).

This story is the rwb-decision app slice of legacy RWB-E2-014 and it owns the ten legacy criteria that the service itself can satisfy, on the approval screen that the legacy story calls SCR-11 with the SCR-18 notice approval panel, reached through SCR-10. The notice is never read as a table: it arrives on the rwb-comms contract of RWB-P6-E1052-CON, and the template code and version on the same answer, so the screen can show the rule and the template that produced the notice without a cross-schema join (DP-01, DP-11). The two ACE facts the screen shows with their business date — the total balance and the postal address — arrive through rwb-source-data (DP-05). The screen model itself is composed at the edge under RWB-PLT-N001-CON, so the field list, the mask flags and the timeout budget of the route are the platform's and are fixed for this screen in RWB-P7-E2014-CON (DP-19).

The approval outcome is the part that has to be right: the outcome, the rule version, the template version, the approver and the business date of every input leave the service as one local decision record through the decision-record library and the shared topic `rwb.decision-record.v1`, and are also written into `approval` and `decision_log_entry` in `rwb_decision` (DP-03). A decision that cannot be recorded does not take effect, which is principle 5 and R-UNI-39, and the notice stays Generated. Age limits, override reasons, templates, variants and role codes stay data rows read through the parameters and reference-data contracts (DP-06, DP-07), so the same screen carries a D7.1 notice of any product without a release.

It serves REQ-APD-002 (the approver sees the recommendation, the reasons, the inputs and the limit that applies on one screen) and REQ-APD-006 (demand and recall notices are approved through approval routing), and supports controls CTL-05 (the preparer cannot decide) and CTL-14 (only an approved notice is dispatched). The measure is notices sent with an approval entry: target 100 percent.

### 4. Flow

1. rwb-comms generates the notice under R-LIT-03 from the current Approved template and raises `rwb.comms.NoticeGenerated.v1`. RWB-P7-E2013-APP consumes it, routes decision D7.1 to role RW-LITOFF and starts `ApprovalWorkflow`; this story does not route.
2. A litigation officer opens SCR-10 Approval queue through the gateway-bff composite route; the queue reads `GET /api/recovery/decision/v1/approvals` with `decision` D7.1, the product filter and the page parameters of the contract.
3. The officer selects the D7.1 item; the route composes `GET /api/recovery/decision/v1/approvals/{approvalId}` with the rwb-comms notice read and the rwb-case case read, one hop each, and serves SCR-11 with the SCR-18 notice approval panel.
4. The screen shows the notice as a document reference with the notice type, the template code and version, the rule ID and version, the preparer, the case number and product group, the total balance with its business date, the addressee role and the postal address masked by the route's mask flags (DP-19).
5. `InputFreshnessCheck` compares the business date of each input with its age limit parameter and reads the template's current status from the notice answer; the screen state becomes READY, STALE_INPUT or the panel state HELD.
6. The officer approves; or picks another Approved template variant of the same notice type and product, enters a coded reason and approves; or refuses with a reason.
7. For a change, the service calls the notice regenerate operation of the rwb-comms contract with the chosen template code; the new notice version replaces the proposed one on the approval and only that version is approved (GAP-1264; ASM-2228).
8. The service checks the decision rules that RWB-P7-E2013-APP fixed — the caller holds Litigation right A, the caller is not the preparer, the reason rules of section 8 — and refuses with the message of section 14 when one fails.
9. The service writes the decision record through the local decision-record library in the same transaction as the approval status, the terms and `decision_log_id`: inputs reference with each input's business date, rule and version, template code and version, approver, final call and decided at. The library relays it on `rwb.decision-record.v1` through the outbox (DP-03, DP-09).
10. On approval the service calls the notice approve operation of the rwb-comms contract so the notice state becomes Approved for the 16:00 dispatch run (JOB-10, RWB-P6-E2021-APP); on refusal it calls the hold operation with reason REFUSED_BY_APPROVER and asks rwb-case for a task in the case owner's queue with the reason.
11. The service signals `ApprovalWorkflow` with the outcome so the escalation timer stops, shows MSG-P7-004 and sets the screen state DECIDED.
12. A decision whose record cannot be written leaves the approval WAITING and the notice Generated, answers 503 with MSG-P7-003 and raises an incident under R-UNI-39.

### 9. State transitions

| From | Event | Condition | To |
|---|---|---|---|
| In progress | Recommendation routed for approval | The notice generated under R-LIT-03 and routed by RWB-P7-E2013-APP | Waiting approval |
| Waiting approval | Approver accepts, or changes and accepts | Not the preparer; the inputs inside their limits; the template current | In progress |
| Waiting approval | Approver refuses | A reason given | In progress, with a task in the case owner's queue |
| Waiting approval | The service level passes | No decision inside the 1 working day of R-LIT-03 | Escalated to RW-HEADLIT by the durable timer of `ApprovalWorkflow` |

The notice state, which is rwb-comms' and is moved by the two calls of this story, moves Generated to Approved on approval and Generated to Held on a refusal or a withdrawn template (R-LIT-04). The approval status column of `approval` follows LOV-APPROVAL-STATUS: WAITING on routing, APPROVED on approval, REFUSED on a refusal, RETURNED on the return operation of API-P7-004, which this screen does not offer. The screen state follows RWB-P7-E2014-CON: READY, STALE_INPUT and DECIDED for SCR-11, and GENERATED, APPROVED and HELD for the SCR-18 panel; the legacy names Ready, Stale input and Decided are kept as the labels of the three shell states.

### 12. Contract

#### 12.1 REST operations provided

Owner rwb-decision (P7); base `/api/recovery/decision/v1`. The shapes are those fixed in RWB-P7-E2014-CON; this story implements them and adds no path.

| ID | Method | Path | Purpose | Access | Idempotency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| API-P7-002 | GET | `/approvals` | The D7.1 queue: the items waiting for the caller's approval level, filtered by decision and product, paged | Decision log, right A, scope applied | Not applicable |
| API-P7-008 | GET | `/approvals/{approvalId}` | One approval with its routing details for the decision screen | Decision log, right A, scope applied | Not applicable |
| API-P7-003 | POST | `/approvals/{approvalId}/approve` | Approve the notice within the caller's authority; answers the decision log entry | Decision log, right A, scope applied | `Idempotency-Key` required |
| API-P7-004 | POST | `/approvals/{approvalId}/return` | Return the item to the preparer with a reason; answers the approval | Decision log, right A, scope applied | `Idempotency-Key` required |
| API-P7-005 | POST | `/approvals/{approvalId}/refuse` | Refuse the notice with a reason; answers the decision log entry | Decision log, right A, scope applied | `Idempotency-Key` required |

The request body of the three decisions is `ApprovalAction` with `reason`, `reasonCode` and `changes.templateCode` for a change of variant, as the legacy contract fixes it. Errors: 401 no session; 403 the role lacks the right (MSG-P4-002); 404 the approval is absent or out of the caller's scope (MSG-P4-003); 409 the preparer (MSG-P7-002), a stale input (MSG-P7-006), a withdrawn template (MSG-P6-003) or a decision already taken (MSG-GEN-004); 422 a missing reason (MSG-GEN-001), a change without a coded reason (MSG-P7-005) or a missing `Idempotency-Key` (MSG-GEN-001); 503 the record was not written (MSG-P7-003) or a consumed call exceeded its budget (MSG-GEN-006). The page size default and maximum of API-P7-002 are fixed in RWB-P7-E2014-CON and are shown on the queue screen as they are declared there.

### 17. Guardrails

- No business value in code: the age limits, the override reasons, the templates, the variants, the approval status values and the route role codes are parameters and reference data read with the version in force (DP-06, DP-07).
- No personal data in events, logs, workflow inputs, tests or prompts: notice IDs, case IDs, template codes, rule versions, user identifiers and dates only, and the address on screen is masked by the route.
- No read of another service's tables and no `v_ace_*` read of this service: the notice, the template, the case, the balance and the contact value arrive on their owners' contracts (DP-01, DP-05, DP-11).
- ACE data only through the owner's contract or the owner's views: only rwb-source-data reads the ACE views, and this service receives the facts it shows on the data-product contract with their business date.
- The approval, the entry and the outbox row commit in one transaction, and a decision that cannot be recorded does not take effect (BR8, R-UNI-39).
- One base, one topic sequence and one queue: `/api/recovery/decision/v1`, `rwb.decision.events.v1` with `rwb.decision.<Event>.v1` names, `rwb.decision-record.v1` for the record and `rwb-decision` in namespace `rwb-recovery` (DP-13, DP-14).
- One hop and 2 s on every call; a timeout never approves a notice, never shows a stale fact as current and never returns an uncleared screen as ready (DP-11).
- No screen field, mask flag or route path is invented here: they are the platform route's and are fixed in RWB-P7-E2014-CON.
- Every example is synthetic; no production payload is copied into a test, a fake or a message.
