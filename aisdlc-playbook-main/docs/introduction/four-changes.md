:::note Section summary
Four core reframes.
:::

Agentic PDLC changes more than the mechanics of software development. It shifts how teams create
value, organise engineering work, measure success and structure delivery:

* Product teams move from delivering fixed scope to continuously improving outcomes.
* Engineering moves from writing every line of code to orchestrating agent-enabled delivery.
* Success moves from effort consumed to business impact realised.
* Large squads give way to smaller, AI-augmented pods supported by reusable context.

These four reframes explain why Agentic PDLC requires a broader change to measures, roles,
commercial models and ways of working, not simply the introduction of another developer tool.

| Reframe | What changes | Practical implication |
|---|---|---|
| **From feature factory to value engine** | Move from time-bound, fixed-scope projects focused on milestones and delivery speed to a continuous, user-centred product lifecycle aligned with business outcomes. | Continuous discovery becomes part of normal delivery. Teams own outcomes, and measures shift from features shipped to value realised. |
| **From writing code to orchestrating AI-powered delivery** | Move from developers producing each artefact and line of code to agents executing and coordinating repeatable work within human-defined architecture, specifications and controls. Quality is designed into specifications before implementation begins. | Developer roles evolve towards architecture, context engineering, orchestration and validation. Delivery capacity scales through reusable agents, specifications and context rather than team size alone. |
| **From paying for time to investing in shared outcomes** | Move from assessing delivery through time, effort and adherence to budget to measuring business outcomes and return on investment. Value is engineered and validated throughout delivery rather than considered only after deployment. | Commercial and delivery measures align more closely to tangible impact, including realised value, time to market and business performance. |
| **From large agile squads to smaller, smarter pods** | Move from large squads built around individual expertise to compact, AI-augmented pods supported by orchestrated agents and codified, reusable context. | Agents handle more repeatable execution while people focus on context, judgement and strategy. Delivery becomes less dependent on individual heroics and more systematic, reusable and repeatable. |

Together, these reframes represent a connected operating-model shift: teams continuously own value,
people govern and orchestrate agent-enabled delivery, success is measured through business impact,
and knowledge is codified so that smaller teams can deliver effectively at scale.
