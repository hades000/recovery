## How measures are used

| How the measures are used |  |
|---|---|
| Applies to every measure. |  |
| Rule | Detail |
| 1 | Measures read from the decision log and the Workbench records once the release that captures them is live |
| 2 | Each measure has one owner who explains the movement each month |
| 3 | Targets by quarter are reviewed at the sponsor review in the last month of each quarter |
| 4 | A measure with no baseline takes its baseline from the first three months of data and its target is set then |
| 5 | The steering dashboard shows every measure by product and route from Epic 2 |
